# ADR-0017: Observability stack: OpenTelemetry instrumentation with a self-hosted or managed backend

- Status: **Proposed** (needs owner approval; blocked on ADR-0002 for telemetry residency)
- Date: 2026-10-10
- Deciders: CTO, Tech Lead, DPO (telemetry can hold personal data)
- Spec refs: section T8, T9, B1, T7, T10 (FinOps)

## Context and problem statement

Spec T8 requires OpenTelemetry traces, metrics and logs with mobile-to-API propagation, PII redaction at the logger, SLOs with multi-window multi-burn-rate alerting, and Grafana-style dashboards. No backend, region or cloud is chosen (ADR-0001, ADR-0002 are Proposed), the team size is unknown (open question 12), and no real personal data may flow yet. The instrumentation standard is settled by the spec; the open question is where telemetry is stored and who operates it. SLO definitions and alert rules in this repo are already vendor-neutral (YAML, generated Prometheus rules).

## Decision drivers

- PDPL residency and transfer: telemetry may carry P2 data by accident, so it follows the ADR-0002 region.
- Small team: operational load of running stateful observability systems.
- Cost per event at marketplace scale, and cost predictability (thin unit economics).
- Portability: avoid a rewrite of instrumentation or alert rules if the vendor changes.
- Alerting on multi-burn-rate rules with runbook links.
- Mobile crash and session data needs a consent gate (B1).

## Considered options

1. **OTel SDKs and Collector, backend: Grafana stack (Prometheus-compatible metrics, Loki, Tempo, Grafana), self-hosted in the chosen region.**
   - Pros: data stays in region, uses the generated Prometheus rules and Grafana JSON as is, no per-event vendor pricing, strong fit with PromQL multi-burn-rate rules.
   - Cons: the team operates storage, upgrades, retention and on-call for the monitoring itself; a monitoring outage coincides with incidents unless isolated.
2. **OTel SDKs and Collector, backend: Grafana Cloud or another managed Prometheus-compatible service.**
   - Pros: no operations, same rules and dashboards, fast start.
   - Cons: residency depends on available regions (VERIFY whether a suitable region exists; likely a transfer issue under B1), usage-based cost, vendor trust and DPA needed.
3. **OTel SDKs, commercial APM (Datadog, New Relic, Honeycomb and similar).**
   - Pros: best out-of-the-box UX, fast time to value, managed.
   - Cons: highest and least predictable cost, residency options vary, SLO and alert rules would need a rewrite into vendor format, stronger lock-in.
4. **Cloud-native only (CloudWatch or equivalent, X-Ray).**
   - Pros: no extra vendor, residency follows cloud region.
   - Cons: depends on the unmade cloud choice, weaker cross-service trace UX and burn-rate tooling, lock-in to the cloud provider.

Pricing and regional availability were not verified in this decision; they are inputs to the owner choice.

## Decision (proposed default)

Instrument with **OpenTelemetry in every case** (SDKs, W3C trace context, OTLP, semantic conventions as in `infra/observability/otel-conventions.md`). For the backend, adopt **option 1 or 2 depending on ADR-0002**:

- If ADR-0002 selects an Egypt-hosted or in-region data plane: option 1, self-hosted Grafana stack in that region, run by a managed Kubernetes or container platform, on isolated infrastructure from the application.
- If ADR-0002 selects a nearby region plus a transfer licence: option 2 only when the managed vendor offers storage in the approved region and is covered by the transfer licence and TIA; otherwise option 1.
- Until then: local development only, using the Collector with a file or console exporter and no real personal data.

Options 3 and 4 are not rejected permanently; the vendor-neutral rules and the OTel layer keep them reachable.

## Consequences

- Alert rules, SLO YAML and dashboards (generated by `tooling/observability/generate.mjs`) are portable to any Prometheus-compatible backend.
- The logger redactor and collector-level attribute processors are mandatory regardless of backend.
- Telemetry retention classes must be set with the DPO against the B1 retention table.
- Self-hosting adds an operational burden; budget on-call time for it, or choose managed once residency allows.
- Paging and incident tooling (on-call scheduler, status page) are a separate decision, not covered here.
