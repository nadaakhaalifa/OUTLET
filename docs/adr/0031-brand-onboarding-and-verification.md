# ADR-0031: Brand onboarding and verification

- Status: Accepted (engineering defaults; legal items stay VERIFY BEFORE LAUNCH)
- Date: 2026-10-11
- Spec: docs/specs/brand-onboarding.md. Contract: docs/specs/brand-onboarding-contract.md. Master prompt: sections 3, 19, B1, B4, B5, B6, 9.5, 27.

## Context
The app opens with "I am a shopper" or "I am a brand". Brands choose local or global, a country, create an account, prove they own the brand, and are accepted, rejected or asked for more information by Z-OUTLET. Accepted brands get a storefront and a dashboard. Existing assets: `kycMachine`, admin KYC queue over `SellerKycAdmin`, stores with kind local|global, audience-scoped tokens.

## Decisions
1. **Separate seller identity, linked by verified phone (not merged).** A person who is already a shopper signs in to the seller audience with the same phone; the system creates a `seller_users` row (own id, own sessions, own strong factor) with a nullable `shopper_user_id` link recorded only after the same phone OTP was verified in both audiences. Options: (a) one account with roles, (b) separate identities. Chose (b): tokens are audience-scoped, a shopper-token compromise must not expose store finance, seller staff are not always shoppers, deleting a shopper account (PDPL, section 2) must not delete a store, and seller auth must demand a strong factor that shoppers do not. Link enables "switch to brand mode" in the app without re-entering the phone; the switch still requires a strong-factor assertion.
2. **Strong factor is mandatory before submit**, not before sign-up: OTP gives an ENROL token (scope `seller:enrol`, 30 min, no refresh) that can only fill a draft; passkey (default) or TOTP (fallback when the device has no passkey support) upgrades to a full session. Submit, document read, and everything on a store need amr containing webauthn or totp.
3. **Application is a first-class resource** (`brand_applications`) layered on the existing KYC machine, not a replacement. One application may produce at most one store. `kycMachine` stays the state authority for the store-level `kyc_status`; application statuses map: DRAFT, SUBMITTED, AUTO_CHECKS, IN_REVIEW (kyc state AUTO_CHECKS with checks finished and a reviewer queue item), NEEDS_INFO, APPROVED, REJECTED, SUSPENDED. WITHDRAWN is application-only. The store row is created at APPROVE, never earlier, so there are no dormant storefronts.
4. **Requirements are data** (`brand_requirement_sets` keyed by kind+country+version, global default fallback), served by `getBrandRequirements`; the UI renders the checklist from it and the API validates submit against the same row. Adding a country is a data change with audit, not a deploy.
5. **Proof of ownership**: Egypt local = commercial register + tax card + signatory ID (21+) + (trademark certificate OR brand authorization/proof of purchase). Global = trademark registration (register + number) OR distributor authorization letter, + company registration extract + signatory ID, + website ownership proof (DNS TXT preferred, or email to admin@/info@/webmaster@/hostmaster@ at the brand domain). Trademark lookup is a port (`TrademarkRegistryPort`) whose result is stored as evidence; absent an adapter it degrades to manual evidence entry (fail closed to human review, never auto-approve).
6. **Humans decide.** Auto checks (scan, OCR consistency, sanctions screen, trademark, domain, duplicate brand, signatory age) only produce evidence and a risk score; APPROVE and REJECT are always an admin decision with reviewer != applicant. This avoids the open legal question on automatic rejection (register row 15 covers suspension, not onboarding). GLOBAL brands from countries in config `brand_onboarding.high_risk_countries` or with any sanctions flag need dual approval (second admin, JIT elevation) before provisioning. Default list is empty plus an OFAC/EU/UN comprehensive-sanctions import; the owner must supply the real list (VERIFY).
7. **Duplicate claims**: first application to reach "domain verified or trademark evidence matched to the applicant" wins priority; the other is routed to trust_safety conflict review (`adminResolveBrandClaimConflict`), never auto-rejected. Brand-name normalization uses casefold + Arabic letter folding + punctuation strip.
8. **Rejection cooldown and appeal**: default 30 days cooldown per normalized (brand name, country, applicant) (configurable per reason, 0 to 365); one appeal within 30 days handled by a different reviewer; no appeal and a 365-day lock for `counterfeit_confirmed` and `sanctions_hit`.
9. **Public vs internal messaging**: applicants see `reason_code`, bilingual public message and requested items; `internal_note` and risk score never leave the admin API.
10. **Provisioning is one transaction + outbox** (store, owner membership, store grants, onboarding checklist, legal-identity projection for B5, `brand_application.approved` event); notifications are consumers (idempotent via inbox). Token claims change on the next refresh; the app forces a refresh when it receives the push or polls `getBrandApplication`.
11. **Documents** live in a private bucket with random keys, signed PUT (15 min), post-upload sha256 + magic-byte check, AV scan, no EXIF/PDF active content, reviewer-only signed GET <= 15 min with a reason code; files of REJECTED/WITHDRAWN applications are purged 30 days after the decision (KYC retention contract+5y applies only to APPROVED stores).

## Consequences
- New module `sellers` (identity, applications, requirements, documents, provisioning) in apps/api; admin module gains the brand-application review use cases behind a `BrandApplicationAdmin` port; `stores` exposes a `ProvisionStore` use case through its public index.
- Seller auth gets its own controller and token audience handling; shopper `/v1/auth/*` is unchanged.
- Open VERIFY items added to the register: sanctions list source, high-risk country list, trademark register API terms, Egyptian national-ID handling for 21+ check, retention of rejected applicants' data.

## Alternatives rejected
- Single account with a role switch: rejected (decision 1).
- Auto-approve on trademark match: rejected; counterfeit risk (master prompt 27, B5 joint liability).
- Create the store at sign-up and gate publishing: rejected; leaves unverified public identity surface and complicates suspension.
