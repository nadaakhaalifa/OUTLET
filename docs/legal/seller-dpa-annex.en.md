# Annex A to the Seller Agreement: Data Processing Addendum (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL/DPO REVIEW. Not in force.** Arabic (`seller-dpa-annex.ar.md`) is authoritative; clause numbers match. The controller/processor roles are a proposal. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-01.

## A1. Roles
A1.1 For buyer delivery data, Z-OUTLET is controller and the seller receives it for the sole purpose of fulfilling the order. Whether the seller acts as processor or independent controller for its own order records is to be confirmed. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-01.
## A2. Scope
A2.1 Data: buyer name, delivery address, masked or relay phone number where supported, order contents. Never: body measurements, budget data, payment instruments.
## A3. Obligations of the seller
A3.1 Use data only to fulfil, ship, support and handle returns for the order. No marketing, resale or profiling.
A3.2 Keep data confidential and secure; limit to staff who need it; use passkey or TOTP.
A3.3 Access ends 30 days after delivery or return closure; delete or anonymise copies except where law requires retention (tax records).
A3.4 Notify Z-OUTLET of any breach without undue delay and within [PLACEHOLDER: hours, shorter than the 72-hour regulatory clock]. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-01.
A3.5 Assist with data-subject requests within [PLACEHOLDER: days].
A3.6 No transfer outside Egypt without Z-OUTLET's written approval and lawful basis. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-02.
A3.7 Sub-processors only with prior written approval.
## A4. Audit
A4.1 Z-OUTLET may request evidence of compliance and audit on reasonable notice.
