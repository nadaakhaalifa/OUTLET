# Z-OUTLET Review Guidelines (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW. Not in force.** Arabic (`review-guidelines.ar.md`) is authoritative; clause numbers match.

## 1. Who can review
1.1 Only buyers of the item (verified purchase). Sellers, their staff and relatives cannot review their own goods.
## 2. What a review should be
2.1 Honest, first-hand, about the product, fit, quality and delivery. Size and fit information is encouraged.
2.2 Photos must be your own and show the product.
## 3. Not allowed
3.1 Fake or paid-for reviews, reviews in exchange for a promise of a positive rating, competitor attacks, personal data of others, hate, harassment, sexual or violent content, advertising, and off-topic content.
## 4. Incentives
4.1 Coins are given for any honest review, whatever the rating; they are never conditional on positivity. Incentivised reviews are marked. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-09.
## 5. Moderation
5.1 Reviews are screened by automated image and text checks (Arabic and English). Removed reviews have a stated reason and may be appealed to a person.
5.2 Reviews are never edited by sellers or by us to change their meaning; sellers may reply publicly.
## 6. Reporting
6.1 Every review has a report button. Law-enforcement and rights-holder requests follow the IP Notice-and-Takedown procedure.
## 7. Your content
7.1 You keep ownership and grant the display licence set out in the Terms of Service clause 9.
