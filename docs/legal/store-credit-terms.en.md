# Z-OUTLET Store Credit Terms (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW. Not in force. Store credit stays limited until the opinion on VR-03 is on file (ADR-0003, ADR-0014).** Arabic (`store-credit-terms.ar.md`) is authoritative; clause numbers match. Wording rule: this document and the app use the word "credit" only.

## 1. What store credit is
1.1 "Z-OUTLET credit" is a closed-loop balance usable only to pay for goods sold on Z-OUTLET. It is a right to future purchases, not a deposit or payment account. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-03.
## 2. How credit is received
2.1 Credit can only arrive as: (a) a refund you choose to take as credit; (b) redemption of a gift card; (c) cashback coins converted to credit; (d) referral credit; (e) goodwill credit from Z-OUTLET.
2.2 You cannot add credit by card, wallet, transfer or any other payment.
## 3. Sub-balances and expiry
| Sub-balance | Expiry |
|---|---|
| Refund credit | None |
| Gift card | Per card, default 3 years (VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-09) |
| Cashback coins | 12 months rolling |
| Referral credit | 6 months |
| Goodwill | 90 days |
3.1 Credit expiring soonest is used first; refund credit is used last. Expiry dates are shown in your balance. Expiry rules must be lawful: VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-09.
## 4. What credit cannot do
4.1 It cannot be withdrawn, paid out to a bank account or mobile wallet, transferred to another person, or used to buy gift cards or Z-OUTLET Plus.
4.2 The only payments outside Z-OUTLET are refunds the law requires to go back to the original payment method; these go through the payment provider and never out of your credit.
## 5. Using credit
5.1 Credit can be combined with one external payment method at checkout. Refund of the part paid with credit returns to the same sub-balance.
5.2 Refunds of instalment (BNPL) orders go back through the provider only, never to credit.
## 6. Account closure
6.1 Before deletion we show your balance and the forfeiture warning. Where the law requires, we offer refund to the original method. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-03, VR-07.
## 7. Misuse
7.1 Credit obtained through fraud may be removed and the account restricted.
## 8. Tax
8.1 VAT treatment of coins and credit is under review. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-B4-04, VR-F1-04.
## 9. Status
9.1 Z-OUTLET credit is not electronic money and is not a payment service. This statement requires written confirmation. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-03.
