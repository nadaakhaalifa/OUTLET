# Z-OUTLET Plus Subscription Terms (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW. Not in force.** Arabic (`plus-subscription-terms.ar.md`) is authoritative; clause numbers match. Prices are admin-configurable defaults, not commitments.

## 1. The plan
1.1 Plans (defaults): Monthly 200 EGP per month; Yearly 800 EGP per year ("pay 4 months, get 8 free", saving 1,600 EGP against 12 x 200 EGP). Prices include VAT. Free trial: 14 days, one per person. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-06.
1.2 Benefits are commerce benefits only: free delivery with no minimum; 24-hour early access and members-only drops; +5% coin cashback; free returns; monthly Plus Day sale and voucher; birthday gift; priority support and delivery slots. The stylist is free for everyone with fair-use limits and is not part of Plus. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-10, VR-23.
## 2. Eligibility
2.1 Plus requires age 21 or older by default. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-08.
## 3. Auto-renewal disclosure (shown before purchase and on every receipt)
3.1 **Your subscription renews automatically** at the end of each period (monthly or yearly) at the price then shown, charged to the saved payment method, until you cancel.
3.2 Before the first charge after a trial we remind you 2 days ahead. Before any price change we notify you at least 30 days ahead.
3.3 Recurring charging depends on provider support. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-04.
## 4. Cancelling
4.1 Cancel in at most two taps: Me > Z-OUTLET Plus > Cancel. No confirmation guilt-wording. You can also pause for 1 to 3 months.
4.2 Benefits continue until the end of the paid period.
## 5. Refunds
5.1 Full refund within 14 days of a charge if no Plus benefit has been used (default policy).
5.2 Pro-rata refunds beyond this, where the law requires. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-07.
5.3 Upgrading from monthly to yearly credits the unused monthly days against the yearly price; downgrades take effect at period end.
## 6. Failed payments
6.1 We retry on days 1, 3 and 7; benefits continue for a 7-day grace period; then the subscription expires.
## 7. Receipts and tax
7.1 An e-receipt is issued for each charge. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-B4-01.
## 8. App Store
8.1 Plus is sold through our payment provider for physical-commerce benefits. If Apple requires in-app purchase for any in-app feature, those features will be sold separately. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-10, VR-23.
## 9. Misuse
9.1 Trial or family-plan abuse (same card or device) may be refused.
