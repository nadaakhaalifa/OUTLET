| Category | Examples | Consent |
|---|---|---|
| Strictly necessary | Session and security tokens, load balancing, fraud prevention, language choice, consent record | None needed; always on |

| Personalisation | Feed and recommendation signals | `personalization` |
| Marketing | Campaign attribution and message performance | `marketing_push`, `marketing_whatsapp`, `marketing_email` |
Error monitoring that carries no identifiers may be treated as necessary. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-01.
