# Z-OUTLET Rewards and Referral Terms (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW. Not in force.** Arabic (`rewards-referral-terms.ar.md`) is authoritative; clause numbers match. Rewards are never cash or money. Games are a Phase 2 feature and stay off until VR-09 is answered.

## 1. Z-OUTLET Coins
1.1 You earn coins for purchases (once delivered and not returned), photo reviews with size information, completing your size profile, fit feedback, referrals, streaks and challenges. Honest reviews earn coins regardless of rating.
1.2 1 coin converts to 1 piastre of store credit (configurable). Coins expire after 12 months. Coins from a returned order are removed.
1.3 Coins and credit cannot be withdrawn, sold or transferred. See Store Credit Terms.
## 2. Tiers
2.1 Bronze, Silver, Gold and Black by net spend over the last 12 months; benefits are shown in the app and may change with notice.
## 3. Games
3.1 Daily spin and post-delivery scratch cards are free to play, never require payment, and award only coins or vouchers.
3.2 The odds of every prize are shown before you play. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-09 (promotional and lottery rules).
3.3 Mystery boxes disclose their value range and a guaranteed minimum value. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-09.
## 4. Referrals
4.1 Friend: 150 EGP off a first order of at least 600 EGP (defaults). Referrer: 100 EGP referral credit after the friend's return window closes and the order is not refunded. 10 successful referrals earn one free Plus month. Maximum 50 rewarded referrals per year.
4.2 The friend must be a new phone, device and payment instrument, and 18+. The referrer needs at least one delivered order.
4.3 Self-referral, multiple accounts and group abuse are rejected without reward and may lead to account restriction.
## 5. Creators and affiliates
5.1 Creator commissions are paid only to verified adults of 21+, with tax details, through a payout. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-17.
## 6. Changes and tax
6.1 We may change or end the programme with notice, honouring earned unexpired coins. VAT treatment of coins is under review. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-B4-04.
