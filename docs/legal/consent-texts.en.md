# Z-OUTLET Consent Texts (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL/DPO REVIEW. Not in force.** Arabic (`consent-texts.ar.md`) is authoritative; clause numbers match. Rules for all: never pre-ticked, one tap to withdraw in Me > Privacy Center, the SDK or feature starts only after consent, refusal never blocks ordering. Wording must be explicit, informed, specific and freely given. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-01.

Each text carries a version id `[PLACEHOLDER: version]` stored in `consent_records`.

## 1. marketing_push
"I agree that Z-OUTLET may send me marketing push notifications about drops, offers and my wishlist (at most 1 a day and 3 a week, not between 23:00 and 09:00). I can withdraw at any time in Privacy Center."
## 2. marketing_whatsapp
"I agree to receive marketing messages from Z-OUTLET on WhatsApp at [PLACEHOLDER: number]. Order messages do not depend on this choice. I can withdraw at any time or reply STOP."
## 3. marketing_email
"I agree to receive marketing emails from Z-OUTLET at my email address. I can unsubscribe in any email or in Privacy Center."
## 4. analytics
"I agree that Z-OUTLET may use pseudonymous analytics about how I use the app to improve it. No analytics tool starts before this consent. Events are kept for 25 months, then aggregated."
## 5. personalization
"I agree that Z-OUTLET may use my views, searches, saves and purchases to personalise my feed, recommendations and stylist. If I decline, I see non-personalised ranking."
## 6. budget_insights
"I agree that Z-OUTLET may store the budget and spending information I enter or that arises from my orders to show my remaining budget and plans. This information is never shared with sellers or used for ads or promotions."
## 7. measurements
"I agree that Z-OUTLET may store my body measurements, encrypted, and use them to recommend sizes. Sellers never see them. I can delete them at any time."
## 8. location_precise
"I agree that Z-OUTLET may use my precise location once or while I use the app to place my address pin and check delivery coverage."
## 9. cross_border_ml
"I agree that data needed for a stylist or fit request may be processed by an AI service located outside [PLACEHOLDER: approved region]. Applies only where a transfer licence exists. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-02."
