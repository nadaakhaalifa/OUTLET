# Z-OUTLET Returns and Refunds Policy (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW. Not in force.** Arabic (`returns-refunds-policy.ar.md`) is authoritative; clause numbers match. Based on Consumer Protection Law 181/2018 as currently understood; all statutory points are unverified. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-07.

## 1. Your right to return
1.1 **Change of mind:** 14 days from the delivery date confirmed by the courier (`delivered_at`), for unused items in original condition with tags and invoice.
1.2 **Defective, not as described or wrong item:** 30 days from the delivery date.
1.3 **Cancellation before shipping:** free, in the app.
1.4 Distance-selling cancellation right (7 days): we treat it as a free return within 7 days of delivery until counsel confirms how it combines with the 14-day right. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-07.
## 2. Items that cannot be returned for change of mind
2.1 Hygiene items (underwear, swimwear without liner, pierced jewellery, opened cosmetics) and personalised items. These are marked on the product page and shown again at checkout. They can be returned if defective. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-07.
2.2 Items sold "as is" with disclosed defects (shown with photos): the disclosed defect is not a return reason; other defects are.
## 3. How to return
3.1 Orders > item > Return or exchange > reason > photos (required for defect and not-as-described) > pickup or drop-off.
3.2 Size reasons are offered an exchange first, with an instant swap where stock exists.
## 4. Who pays return shipping
4.1 Change of mind: you (free for Plus members; first size exchange is free). Defective, not as described, wrong item: the seller.
4.2 For change of mind, the original delivery fee is refundable only if the whole order is returned within 7 days. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-07.
## 5. Inspection
5.1 The seller or Z-OUTLET hub inspects within 48 hours of receipt; silence counts as approval. A rejection must include photos and you can dispute it. A person at Z-OUTLET reviews disputes within 5 business days.
## 6. Refunds
6.1 We start your refund within 24 hours of receiving the return. We refund within 14 days. The statutory starting date (delivery or return receipt) is under review and we apply the stricter reading meanwhile. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-07 (see ADR-0009).
6.2 Refund destination: by default the original payment method.
| Paid with | Refund goes to |
|---|---|
| Card, Apple Pay, Meeza | Original card/wallet via the payment provider (5-14 business days) |
| Mobile wallet, InstaPay | Reversal where supported, else transfer to your verified account. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-04 |
| Fawry code | Store credit or Fawry refund voucher |
| Instalments (BNPL) | **Through the instalment provider only** (cancellation or partial cancellation); never as store credit |
| Cash on delivery | Store credit instantly if you choose, or bank/wallet transfer. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-F1-02 |
| Store credit or gift card | Back to the same balance |
6.3 Where both are lawful, you choose between original method and instant store credit.
6.4 Partial refunds include the proportional voucher share; delivery fee is refunded where the seller is at fault.
## 7. Exceptions and fairness
7.1 Accounts with a return rate above 60% over at least 10 orders in 6 months may lose free returns, with notice. This policy is published and not secret.
7.2 Return fraud may lead to refusal and restriction.
