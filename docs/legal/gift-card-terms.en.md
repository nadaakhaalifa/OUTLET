# Z-OUTLET Gift Card Terms (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT. Gift-card sales and redemption are behind a kill switch that stays OFF until VR-03 and VR-06 are verified or accepted in writing (ADR-0003, ADR-0014).** Arabic (`gift-card-terms.ar.md`) is authoritative; clause numbers match.

## 1. Scope
1.1 A gift card is a prepaid right to buy goods on Z-OUTLET, platform-wide or limited to one store. Sale and redemption are currently not available. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-03.
## 2. Buying
2.1 Amounts 100 to 10,000 EGP. Buyers must be 18+; purchases above 5,000 EGP and corporate purchases require 21+. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-08.
2.2 Group gifts: contributors pay into a pot; the card is issued when the pot closes and unfunded contributions are refunded.
2.3 Gift cards cannot be bought with store credit, other gift cards or Plus benefits.
## 3. Redeeming
3.1 Redemption converts the card into the gift-card sub-balance of the recipient's Z-OUTLET credit. Partial use is allowed.
3.2 Codes are secret. Five wrong attempts per hour per account are blocked temporarily. Z-OUTLET is not responsible for codes shared with others.
## 4. Expiry
4.1 Default validity is 3 years from issue, shown on the card. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-09, VR-F1-04.
## 5. Limits
5.1 Not exchangeable for cash, not withdrawable, not transferable for payment and not refundable once redeemed, except where the law requires. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-07.
5.2 Lost or stolen cards: report to support; we may void and reissue where the code has not been redeemed.
## 6. Tax
6.1 VAT point (sale or redemption) is under review. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-B4-04, VR-F1-04, VR-06.
## 7. Personal data
7.1 Recipient contact details are used only to deliver the gift and are deleted after 30 days.
