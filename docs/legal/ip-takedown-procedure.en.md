## 1. Who may notify
1.1 Rights holders (or authorised agents) of trademarks, copyright, designs and other IP, through the brand-owner portal or [PLACEHOLDER: IP notices email].
## 2. Content of a notice
2.1 Identity and contact of the rights holder and agent authority; identification of the right (registration number where applicable); listing URL or ID; explanation of the infringement; statement of good faith and accuracy; signature.
## 3. Our response
3.1 We acknowledge receipt, and aim to act within 48 hours: remove or disable the listing and notify the seller with the notice.
3.2 Serious or repeat infringement leads to suspension under the Seller Agreement, with human review of suspensions. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-15.
## 4. Counter-notice
4.1 The seller may reply with proof of authenticity or authorisation within [PLACEHOLDER: days]. We forward the reply to the rights holder, who may confirm, withdraw or go to the competent authority or court. We restore the listing only if the rights holder does not pursue within [PLACEHOLDER: days]. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-07.
## 5. False notices
5.1 Knowingly false notices may lead to account restriction and legal liability.
## 6. Records and privacy
6.1 We keep records for [PLACEHOLDER: period] and share the notifier's details with the seller only as necessary. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-01.
