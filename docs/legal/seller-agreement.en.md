## 5. Prices, genuine discounts and monitoring
5.1 You must not advertise a fake reference price. Z-OUTLET computes a Verified Reference Price from evidence and shows a percentage-off claim only when verified. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-07.
5.2 **Price parity:** your Z-OUTLET price must not exceed your price on other channels. A cheaper external price leads to demotion, 48 hours to fix, then automatic pausing of the item.
5.3 **Consent to monitoring:** you consent to Z-OUTLET reading your public prices, availability and listings via your connected channels and a polite crawler respecting robots.txt, for price verification. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-01, VR-13.
5.4 Prices and stock may not be edited after a drop is scheduled except to increase stock.
## 6. Automatic penalties and suspension
| Event | Automatic action |
|---|---|
| Insufficient evidence | Percentage-off hidden |
| Claim above reference | Corrected display; you are notified with evidence |
| Pre-drop inflation | Badge removed 30 days; ranking penalty |
| Cheaper elsewhere | Demotion, 48h, pause |
| 3 violations in 90 days | Verified badge lost; commission surcharge [PLACEHOLDER]; drops blocked 30 days |
| Severe or repeated | Store suspended; payouts held as set out in clause 8 |
6.1 You may appeal by connecting an integration or uploading machine-readable evidence; the claim is re-scored automatically.
6.2 **Human review:** every suspension is reviewed by a person within 5 business days of your request. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-15 (legality of automatic suspension) and the penalty clauses' enforceability (VR-07).
## 7. Stock, shipping, returns
7.1 Stock sync must be accurate; oversell penalties apply. Items must be as described, with defects disclosed and photographed.
7.2 You follow the Returns and Refunds Policy; inspection within 48 hours; silence is approval. Defective returns are at your cost. A refund after payout is netted from your next payout.
## 8. Payouts
8.1 Weekly cycle; a line becomes eligible after the return window closes plus 3 days and no dispute. Payouts go to the verified IBAN, name-matched, and large payouts need dual approval.
8.2 Z-OUTLET may hold payouts for risk, disputes, suspension or negative balance.
8.3 Collection and payout model: through the payment provider's marketplace/sub-merchant product; Z-OUTLET does not pool your funds. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-22, VR-03, VR-04, VR-PO-01.
