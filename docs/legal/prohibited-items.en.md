# Z-OUTLET Prohibited Items Policy (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW. Not in force.** Arabic (`prohibited-items.ar.md`) is authoritative; clause numbers match.

## 1. Principle
1.1 Sellers may list only lawful, genuine goods that fit the Z-OUTLET catalogue (fashion, accessories, footwear, bags, beauty and home items as configured). Listings must have an Arabic title and description.
## 2. Prohibited
2.1 Counterfeit, replica or unauthorised branded goods, or goods without proof of authenticity or authorisation.
2.2 Goods illegal in Egypt or whose sale needs a licence the seller does not hold: weapons, drugs, tobacco and vapes, alcohol, medicines and regulated medical devices, protected wildlife products, stolen goods. [PLACEHOLDER: counsel to complete list]. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-07.
2.3 Goods or text that are hateful, sexually explicit, violent or that exploit minors.
2.4 Used or pre-loved goods (not yet supported) and items with undisclosed defects. Sample and minor-defect items must be labelled and photographed.
2.5 Personal data, accounts, vouchers, gift cards or services unrelated to goods.
2.6 Hazardous goods that couriers do not carry.
## 3. Restricted
3.1 Hygiene goods (underwear, swimwear, cosmetics) need disclosed non-return status. Personalised goods must be labelled.
## 4. Enforcement
4.1 Listings are removed; repeat or serious breaches lead to suspension under the Seller Agreement with human review of suspensions. VERIFY WITH COUNSEL: VR-15.
4.2 Report a listing from its page; we aim to act on counterfeit notices within 48 hours.
