# Egyptian fashion e-commerce market and consumer behaviour, 2024–2026

Research method note: these notes come from web search snippets and secondary reporting, written 2026-11-06. datareportal.com could not be fetched from this session, so the DataReportal figures are from search summaries only. Most market-sizing figures come from commercial research vendors with paywalled methods, so treat them as weak.

## 1. Market size: total e-commerce, fashion e-commerce, offline apparel, penetration

### Takeaway
The most cited estimate for Egypt's total B2C e-commerce in 2025 is about USD 10 billion, growing at roughly 10–14% a year to about USD 19–21 billion by 2030. Fashion e-commerce estimates disagree by more than 2x: about USD 0.3–0.8 billion in 2025, growing at roughly 11% a year. No official MCIT or CAPMAS figure was found.

### Cited Findings
- Total e-commerce in 2025: USD 10.24bn, rising to USD 19.28bn by 2030 (13.49% CAGR). Vendor estimate, methodology unclear. — [MEA TechWatch](https://meatechwatch.com/?p=31581)
- Mordor Intelligence, 2025: USD 10.39bn, rising to USD 20.72bn by 2030. Vendor estimate, paywalled. — [IMARC/Mordor roundup via search](https://imarcgroup.com/egypt-e-commerce-market)
- ResearchAndMarkets B2C databook: USD 9.91bn in 2025, about 10% CAGR through 2029, and USD 14.53bn by 2029. It names Amazon and Noon as the anchor marketplaces, with B.Tech, Carrefour and Jumia following omnichannel strategies. — [ValueSpectrum/GlobeNewswire](https://www.valuespectrum.com/corporate_news/1376512-egypt-b2c-ecommerce-databook-report-2025-a-14-53-billion-market-by-2029-amazon-and-noon-anchor-marketplaces-while-b-tech-carrefour-egypt-and-jumia-leverage-omnichannel-control)
- IMARC: a 2024 size of USD 93.7 *million* and an 8.36% CAGR. This is almost certainly a units error, so discard it. — [IMARC](https://imarcgroup.com/egypt-e-commerce-market)
- Fashion e-commerce, 2025 estimates:
  - Credence Research: USD 814.5m, with a 10.75% CAGR for 2026–2035. — [Credence Research](https://www.credenceresearch.com/report/egypt-e-commerce-fashion-and-apparel-market)
  - Ken Research: about USD 820m, rising to USD 1,702m by 2032 (about 11% CAGR). — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/egypt-e-commerce-fashion-apparel-market.md)
  - ECDB: USD 401m, with apparel at 59% of fashion revenue. — [ECDB](https://ecdb.com/resources/sample-data/market/eg/fashion)
  - ecommerceDB: USD 311m in 2025 (apparel USD 185.1m). — [ecommerceDB](https://ecommercedb.com/markets/eg/fashion)
- Recommerce (second-hand) in 2025: USD 683m, growing 16.3%. Vendor estimate. — [ValueSpectrum](https://www.valuespectrum.com/corporate_news/1290837-egypt-recommerce-intelligence-report-2025-market-to-grow-by-16-3-to-reach-683-million-this-year-transaction-value-volume-and-market-share-trends-2020-2029)
- Apparel retail grew in 2024 despite devaluation and inflation. Consumers traded down, and local brands with competitively priced collections gained traction. — [Euromonitor, Apparel and Footwear Specialists in Egypt, Apr 2025 (paywalled abstract)](https://www.marketresearch.com/Euromonitor-International-v746/Apparel-Footwear-Specialists-Egypt-40786698)

### Inferences
- The two fashion clusters (about USD 0.3–0.4bn from ECDB and ecommerceDB, about USD 0.8bn from Credence and Ken) probably differ in scope: footwear and bags, marketplaces versus D2C, and whether social commerce is counted. A planning range of about USD 0.4–0.8bn (roughly EGP 20–40bn at about EGP 48–50/USD; the FX rate is assumed, not sourced) is defensible.
- If total e-commerce is about USD 10bn, fashion's share is about 4–8%.

### Gaps
- No credible figure was found for the offline apparel market or for online penetration of apparel. Euromonitor has these figures but they are paywalled.
- No official government e-commerce statistic was found.

## 2. Payments: COD, wallets, InstaPay, Fawry, BNPL

### Takeaway
Cash on delivery is still the largest online payment method, but estimates of its share range from 45% to over 85% and none is authoritative. Digital rails are growing fast: InstaPay handled about 263m transactions worth EGP 1.2tn in Q1 2025; active mobile wallets reached 46.3m in Q2 2025, with about 60m registered by the end of 2025; and valU, the BNPL leader, posted FY2025 GMV of EGP 24.5bn.

### Cited Findings
- COD share estimates:
  - 45% of e-commerce transactions (2024), from a vendor guide. — [EasyApps guide](https://easyappsecom.com/uk/guides/selling-on-shopify-egypt)
  - More than 60% of purchases, from a market report. — [Renub](https://www.renub.com/egypt-e-commerce-market-p.php)
  - Over 90%, undated. — [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments/expert-views/egypts-still-nascent-ecommerce-market-now-drawing-global-interest)
  - The Jumia Egypt CEO was quoted saying COD was 85% of payments, date unclear. Jumia cut cash to 51% during one campaign. — [Amwal Al Ghad (AR)](https://amwalalghad.com/0v2k)
- InstaPay/IPN:
  - Q1 2025: 263m transactions worth over EGP 1.2tn, per the CBE. — [Arab Finance](https://arabfinance.com/en/news/newdetails/29811)
  - 2024: about 1.5bn transactions worth EGP 2.6–2.9tn. Secondary reports, with conflicting values. — [Egyptian Streets](https://egyptianstreets.com/?p=98009)
  - Users: about 12m at the end of 2024 (the most-cited figure). A claim of 16m by mid-2025 comes only from an unverified LinkedIn post.
  - A fee of 0.1% (min EGP 0.50, max EGP 20) started on 1 April 2025. — [Egyptian Streets](https://egyptianstreets.com/2025/04/03/instapay-now-charging-transfer-fees)
  - InstaPay turned profitable a year after the fees began, with no significant drop in adoption. — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/north-africa/egypt-instapay-turns-profitable-a-year-after-transfer-fee-rollout-rt18pyrt)
- Mobile wallets (NTRA):
  - Q1 2025: 43.7m wallets, up from 33.3m a year earlier. — [NTRA](https://www.tra.gov.eg/en/national-telecommunications-regulatory-authority-ntra-issues-report-on-mobile-wallet-usage-indicators-for-q1-2025-press-releases-13-may-2025/)
  - Q2 2025: 46.3m active wallets (+29% y/y) and about 718m transactions (+80%) worth EGP 943.4bn (+72%). Vodafone Cash held 55% of wallets, 78% of transactions and 81% of value; e& Cash 21%, Orange Cash 19%, WE Pay 5%. — [NTRA](https://www.tra.gov.eg/en/ntra-issues-q2-2025-report-on-the-usage-indicators-of-mobile-wallets-operated-by-telecom-companies/), [Daily News Egypt](https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/?p=837245)
  - End of 2025: about 60m registered e-wallets (versus 15.2m in 2019) and about EGP 4tn in 2025 wallet transactions, per the CBE. — [Business Today Egypt](https://www.businesstodayegypt.com/amp/4/7346/Egypt-mobile-wallet-transactions-hit-EGP-4T-as-user-base)
- BNPL (valU):
  - H1 2025: GMV EGP 10.6bn (+80%) on 3.6m transactions (+133%). — [Mubasher](https://english1.mubasher.info/news/4486620/MENA-focused-Valu-s-net-income-surges-64-in-H1-25/{{attachment.url}})
  - 9M 2025: GMV EGP 17.32bn on 6.1m transactions, about 23% consumer-finance market share. — [valU 9M25 deck](https://www.valu.com.eg/pdfs/Investor%20Presentation%209M25.pdf)
  - FY2025: revenue EGP 5.6bn, net income EGP 764m (+81%), GMV EGP 24.5bn. The 8.7m transactions figure comes from a headline only and was not verified. — [TechAfrica News](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/12/valu-reports-record-fy25-performance-with-71-revenue-growth-and-8-7-million-transactions/)

### Inferences
- valU's roughly 23% share implies total FRA-regulated consumer finance of about EGP 100bn+ GMV in 2025. This is a derived figure.
- Wallets and InstaPay are now mass-market, so offering them at checkout is required, not optional. COD will still be needed at launch.

### Gaps
- No fashion-specific COD share.
- No 2025 data for Fawry, Sympl, Aman, Contact, Souhoola or Halan.
- No authoritative full-year 2025 InstaPay figures or user count.

## 3. Inflation, devaluation and trading down

### Takeaway
After the 2022–2024 devaluations, including the March 2024 float, inflation peaked at about 38% in 2023 and fell to about 10% in January 2026. It then rose again to 13–15% for most of 2026. Clothing inflation has stayed in double digits (about 12.6–14% y/y in mid-2026). Consumers are trading down to cheaper local brands and discount channels, which supports an off-price model.

### Cited Findings
- Headline annual inflation was 10.1% in January 2026. — [Egypt Today](https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/144973/Egypt’s-annual-inflation-rate-drops-to-10-1-in-January)
- Urban inflation in 2026:
  - February: 13.4%. — [Daily News Egypt](https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/03/10/egypts-urban-inflation-rises-to-13-4-in-february-capmas/)
  - March: 15.2%. — [Business Today Egypt](https://www.businesstodayegypt.com/amp/1/7278/Egypt’s-urban-inflation-jumps-to-15-2-in-March-2026)
  - May: 14.6%. — [Scene Now](https://scenenow.com/News/Egypt-s-Annual-Urban-Inflation-Slows-to-14-6-in-May)
  - July: 14.9%. — [Daily News Egypt](https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/08/10/egypts-annual-urban-inflation-accelerates-to-14-9-in-july-from-14-3-in-june/)
  - September: 13.9%, down from 14.5% in August. — [Daily News Egypt](https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/10/10/egypts-annual-urban-inflation-eases-to-13-9-in-september/)
- Clothing and footwear inflation:
  - Urban: 13.6% y/y in June 2026 (May 14.2%).
  - Nationwide: 13% in July and 12.6% in August 2026. In August, fabrics rose 14.3% and ready-made clothing 13.4%. — [Ahram (Aug)](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/576399.aspx), [Ahram (Jul)](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/574328.aspx)
  - September 2026 month-on-month: fabrics +1.7%, ready-made clothing +0.6%, footwear +1.2%. — [Zawya](https://zawya.com/en/economy/egypts-annual-urban-inflation-eases-to-13.9-in-september-1587961)
- The CBE expects inflation to stabilise in Q3 2026, then decline towards its target of 7% ±2 pp. — [Zawya](https://zawya.com/en/economy/egypts-annual-urban-inflation-eases-to-13.9-in-september-1587961)
- Trading down (2024): see Euromonitor in §1.
- In 2023, 43% of Egyptian respondents said they would shop more at discount stores to save money (second-highest among the MENA countries surveyed). — [Statista](https://WWW.STATISTA.COM/statistics/1380604/mena-share-of-consumers-shopping-more-at-discount-stores-as-a-saving-strategy-by-country/)

### Inferences
- Apparel prices have compounded by roughly 60–100%+ since 2022. This is a rough estimate, not sourced. It makes verified discounts and budget tools especially valuable.
- Ongoing price rises make "was" prices easy to inflate. Machine-verified reference prices (VRP) are a real differentiator.

### Gaps
- The October 2026 CAPMAS release was not found.
- The 2023 inflation peak figure is from memory and needs a source.
- No Egypt-specific survey measuring apparel trade-down rates was found.

## 4. Digital adoption, devices, demographics, social commerce

### Takeaway
Egypt has about 96m internet users (January 2025) and about 51.6m social media identities (October 2025), and its population is young (median age about 25). Android dominates with about 88–91% of mobile OS share, against iOS at about 9–12% (2026). An iOS-first launch therefore reaches only a small, higher-income slice of the market.

### Cited Findings
- January 2025: 96.3m internet users (+12m, +14.6% y/y) and 50.7m social media identities (43.1% of the population). — [DataReportal Digital 2025 Egypt](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-egypt)
- October 2025: 51.6m social media identities (43.4%). — [DataReportal Digital 2026 Egypt](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-egypt)
- Egypt added 7.9m mobile internet users in 2025. — [MEA TechWatch](https://meatechwatch.com/?p=51225)
- Mobile OS share: Android 88.01% / iOS 11.99% (August 2026) and Android 91.09% / iOS 8.89% (June 2026). — [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/egypt)
- Population: about 118.4m (2025) and 120.1m (2026), per UN WPP 2024 estimates. Median age about 25.3–25.7, with about 32% under 15. CAPMAS uses a lower resident count. — [Worldometers](https://www.worldometers.info/world-population/egypt-population/), [StatisticsTimes](https://www.statisticstimes.com/demographics/country/egypt-demographics.php)
- Academic work (n=400, 2024) finds social media drives fashion purchase intention in Egypt. — [EKB journal](https://journals.ekb.eg/article_373561.html)

### Inferences
- iOS users are likely the urban, higher-income segment, which suits Apple Pay and card payments and means less COD. Android should follow quickly, as the spec plans.

### Gaps
- No per-platform ad reach (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp), because DataReportal was blocked.
- No figure for the number of people who buy through Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp commerce.

## 5. Price sensitivity, deal-seeking, fake discounts, trust

### Takeaway
Shoppers are highly price-sensitive. Sales events such as White Friday often draw sceptical reactions that prices stay high despite the advertised discounts. Hard data on how Egyptian shoppers view fake discounts is scarce.

### Cited Findings
- White Friday coverage: high discounts, yet prices remain unaffordable and buyers delay non-essential purchases. This is older coverage. — [Amwal Al Ghad](https://en.amwalalghad.com/?p=65791)
- 43% would shift to discount stores (2023). — [Statista](https://WWW.STATISTA.COM/statistics/1380604/mena-share-of-consumers-shopping-more-at-discount-stores-as-a-saving-strategy-by-country/)

### Inferences
- Common suspicion that discounts are inflated supports the "verified discount" positioning.

### Gaps
- No quantitative Egyptian survey on trust in discounts or fake discounts was found. Consumer Protection Agency complaint statistics would be the best source.

## 6. Sizing and returns

### Takeaway
No Egypt-specific data on return rates or sizing problems was found. The planning baseline relies on global figures, where fit causes more than 50% of apparel returns, and a 2020 Egyptian academic paper citing 14–40% online fashion return rates.

### Cited Findings
- Online fashion return rates of 14–40%, as cited in an Egyptian university paper (2020, secondary). — [MIU Egypt](https://cscgp.miuegypt.edu.eg/?p=8903)
- Size and fit cause more than 50% of US apparel returns (Coresight). — [Chain Store Age](https://chainstoreage.com/technology/survey-apparel-has-an-online-returns-problem)

### Inferences
- COD refusal at the door probably functions as Egypt's main "return" channel, which the spec's §20.7 already treats as a guardrail.

### Gaps
- No Egyptian fashion return rates, size-return shares or courier RTO data. The courier data is listed as a VERIFY item in the spec register.
