# Competitive landscape: online fashion and off-price shopping in Egypt and MENA (as of 2026-11-06)

Method note: there were 12 web searches and no page fetches, so the findings below rest on search snippets. Every figure is labelled with its year. Anything marked UNVERIFIED needs a primary-source check before anyone relies on it.

## 1. Horizontal marketplaces: Noon, Namshi, Amazon.eg, Jumia Egypt

### Takeaway
The generalist marketplaces in Egypt are Jumia, Noon and Amazon.eg. Fashion is one category among many for them, and none of them runs a verified-discount off-price proposition. Jumia pulled back in Egypt in 2025 and moved its sourcing toward Yiwu (China), which puts it closer to cheap goods than to branded clearance.

### Cited Findings
- **Jumia, 2025:** Q1 2025 revenue fell 26%, mainly because of lower corporate sales in Egypt and currency devaluation. Q3 2025 showed the same driver. — [TechAfricaNews](https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/08/jumia-q1-2025-revenue-falls-26-orders-surge-21-amid-strategic-focus/); [Jumia Q2 2025 release](https://s205.q4cdn.com/370993272/files/doc_financials/2025/q2/Jumia-EX-99-1-Q2-2025-8-6-2025-v2-FINAL-1-1.pdf)
- **Jumia, Egypt corporate sales:** Jumia deliberately deprioritised corporate sales in Egypt in 2025. Management (CEO Francis Dufay) said Egypt revenue was "increasing weekly". This is a management claim, not a verified result. — [Insider Monkey Q3 2025 call](https://www.insidermonkey.com/blog/jumia-technologies-ag-nysejmia-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript-1644706)
- **Jumia, FY2025:** revenue was about $188.9m (+13%) and GMV about $818.6m. It is reported to have missed its own order and GMV guidance. Jumia is targeting break-even in 2026. Fashion is named as a "strong demand" category, but no Egypt fashion share is disclosed. — [Yahoo Finance Q4 2025](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/jumia-jmia-q4-2025-earnings-131816413.html); [Pluboard, Feb 2026](https://pluboard.com/2026/02/10/companies/earnings-and-dividend/jumia-break-even-2026-nigeria-growth-losses)
- **Jumia, sourcing:** Jumia dropped premium brands in favour of affordable fashion, electronics and home goods sourced directly from Yiwu. — [WeeTracker](https://weetracker.com/?p=87216)
- **Noon:** Noon entered Egypt in February 2019. — [Enterprise](https://enterprise.press/?p=49952)
- **Noon, Egypt seller fees (effective 1 Feb 2026):** apparel and footwear 15% of the sale price; watches and eyewear 12%. Noon also publishes FBP fulfilment fees for Egypt. — [noon partners help center](https://support.noon.partners/portal/en/kb/articles/fulfilled-by-partner-fbp-fees-in-egypt)
- **Namshi:** Emaar sold Namshi to Noon for AED 1.23bn (US$335.2m) in 2022. Namshi runs independently but uses Noon's logistics. Namshi announced plans to enter Egypt in 2018–19, and I found no source confirming it currently operates in Egypt. — [The National 2022](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/markets/2022/08/25/emaar-signs-deal-with-noon-to-sell-namshi-for-335m); [Menabytes](https://www.menabytes.com/namshi-egypt/)
- **Amazon.eg:** runs regular tentpole sales: Prime Day (25–31 July), 11.11 (7–12 November) and the Eid sale with "up to 50%". — [Zawya Prime Day](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/seven-days-epic-savings-and-deep-discounts-amazoneg-announces-prime-day-from-july-25-to-31-exclusively-for-prime-members-across-egypt-jkm54wap); [Zawya 11.11](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/amazoneg-announces-1111-sale-event-with-hundreds-of-thousands-of-deals-from-november-7-to-12-ppt6z8wc); [Egypt Today](https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/6/131281/Amazon-Eg-Announces-‘Eid-Sale’-with-Savings-up-to-50)
- **Amazon.eg, seller fees (launch era, about 2021, UNVERIFIED as current):** referral fee of at least 3%, varying by category; EGP 5 per item on items under EGP 100; storage fees extra. — [Scoop Empire](https://scoopempire.com/online-shopping-is-about-to-get-a-whole-lot-easier-with-amazon-eg-launching-later-this-year)

### Inferences
- Noon's 15% on apparel (2026) is a practical reference ceiling for Outlet NK's commission.
- The incumbents' "up to X% off" event marketing is not evidence-based. A verified-reference-price position therefore differentiates Outlet NK, and it is defensible under Consumer Protection Law 181/2018.
- Jumia's move toward Yiwu goods leaves the branded clearance segment less contested.

### Gaps
- Current Amazon.eg fashion referral %, delivery fees, free-delivery thresholds and return windows were not found.
- I found no 2025–26 source for Jumia Egypt or Noon Egypt delivery fees and return policies (Noon's return policy was not checked).
- No source gave an Egypt fashion share for any marketplace.

## 2. SHEIN, Temu and Trendyol

### Takeaway
The cross-border ultra-cheap players reach Egypt, but I could not verify their entry dates, customs pushback or app rankings. Trendyol is strong in the GCC, and I found no confirmed Egypt launch.

### Cited Findings
- **Temu:** listed in 2025 as shipping to Egypt directly from its global warehouses. — [Cairo360](https://www.cairo360.com/?p=1788197)
- **Egypt customs, air cargo:** the ACI advance-registration system became mandatory for all air shipments from January 2026, as announced in November 2025. At the end of 2025, Egypt cut automation and e-service fees on inbound air shipments by USD 80. These rules plausibly raise friction for cross-border parcels, but I found no source that names SHEIN or Temu. — [Amwal Al Ghad](https://en.amwalalghad.com/tag/aci-system)
- **Trendyol:** launched its Gulf app in August 2023, has offices in Riyadh and Dubai, reports more than 13m downloads and more than 4m GCC customers, and is described as Alibaba-backed. — [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/2576175/amp); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/trendyol-vs-yashry)
- **Yashry:** a Cairo-based fashion marketplace founded in 2019. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/trendyol-vs-yashry)

### Gaps
- SHEIN's Egypt entry date, any local warehouse, Egyptian customs or regulatory actions against SHEIN or Temu, their pricing in EGP, and Sensor Tower or data.ai rankings for Egypt were all not found. These are UNVERIFIED.

## 3. Off-price, flash-sale and outlet players, past and present

### Takeaway
I found no confirmed Egyptian online off-price or flash-sale marketplace in 2026. The GCC off-price leader (BFL) has stated ambitions in Africa but has no confirmed presence in Egypt. Brantu, the last funded Egyptian fashion marketplace, appears to have pivoted.

### Cited Findings
- **Brands For Less (BFL):** a Lebanon/UAE off-price chain with more than 120 stores across the GCC and Europe (2024). In 2023 it said it was looking at Africa and Asia, and it is pushing e-commerce. There is no Egypt store or e-commerce operation confirmed. — [AGBI 2023](https://www.agbi.com/retail/2023/09/uaes-brands-for-less-sets-sights-on-asia-and-africa/); [Zawya 2024](https://zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/brands-for-less-group-reaches-new-milestone-with-more-than-120-stores-in-the-gcc-and-europe-brg7g9cr)
- **Brantu:** launched in July 2019 as a multi-brand fashion marketplace (local and global brands) and raised a seven-figure Series A led by Sawari Ventures in 2020. Its predecessor, Elprices, shut down in early 2019. CB Insights now describes it as a creator and social-commerce platform. That suggests a pivot, but the date is unknown. — [Wamda 2020](https://www.wamda.com/2020/11/brantu-raises-series-investment); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/brantu)
- **Egyptian retail stress:** several top retail brands were reported to be closing stores in Egypt in January 2024, which points to a potential source of excess stock. — [Egyptian Streets](https://egyptianstreets.com/2024/01/17/top-retail-brands-in-egypt-to-shut-stores-reports/)

### Gaps
- The following were not researched or found: 6thStreet outlet, Sivvi, Ubuy, Dresscode, Ezzy, Sooqna, the fates of Sukar and JadoPado, Ounass, Max, Defacto, LC Waikiki, Town Team, Concrete, and Egyptian outlet malls (Cairo Festival City, Mall of Egypt, Stella Di Mare factory outlets). Failure reasons for past players are therefore unverified.

## 4. Social commerce (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp)

### Takeaway
Social commerce is widely described as a major fashion channel in Egypt, but the market-size figures conflict wildly and are low quality.

### Cited Findings
- Market-size claims conflict:
  - "Social commerce more than $15bn by 2024" (a 2022 claim). — [ContentEngine](https://contentenginellc.com/?p=356245)
  - Egypt e-commerce fashion and apparel market of $4.5bn. — [Ken Research](https://www.marketresearch.com/Ken-Research-v3771/Egypt-Commerce-Fashion-Apparel-42965104)
  - Total Egypt e-commerce of about $101.6m in 2025, which is clearly implausible. — [MolecularCloud](https://www.molecularcloud.org/p/egypt-e-commerce-market-growth-size-trends-and-forecast-2026-2034)
  - Treat all three as unreliable.
- **Social-commerce enablers are funded:** Taager raised $6.75m pre-Series B (Feb 2025), Brimore raised $25m (2022, IFC-led), and Wuilt, a store builder, raised $2m (Aug 2025). — [Gulf Africa Review](https://www.gulfafricareview.com/taager-secures-funding-to-propel-social-e-commerce-in-mena); [TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/01/30/egyptian-social-commerce-startup-brimore-raises-25m-led-by-the-ifc-and-endure-capital/embed); [Enterprise 2025](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2025/08/05/wuilt-raises-usd-2-mn-in-funding-to-take-e-commerce-model-regional)

### Inferences
- Local Instagram brands are a supply pool (their overstock) more than a competitor. Outlet NK's channel integrations and Wuilt-style storefront links could onboard them.

### Gaps
- No reliable share of fashion sold via Instagram or WhatsApp, no COD share and no average order value were found.

### Cited Findings

- **noon One:** officially available in the UAE and KSA only (from SAR 29/month). A third-party site lists a "noon VIP" in Egypt at EGP 300/year, which is UNVERIFIED. — [noon help](https://help.noon.com/portal/en/kb/articles/how-much-does-noon-one-cost); [Subger](https://subger.com/ar/service/noon-eg-market)

### Inferences
- The master prompt's Plus pricing of EGP 200/month and EGP 800/year is about 7× Amazon Prime's monthly price and 2.5× talabat pro's. Prime anchors Egyptian price expectations at about EGP 250/year. This is a pricing risk to raise with the user, because pricing is an "ask first" decision.

### Gaps
- No traction or member numbers were found for any of these programs.

## 6. Funding of Egyptian e-commerce, 2024–2026

### Cited Findings
- Egypt VC funding was $185m in 1H 2025 (+90% y-o-y, MAGNiTT). E-commerce was the #2 funded sector in the Middle East at $321m. — [Enterprise, Jul 2025](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2025/07/20/vc-funding-in-egypt-hits-usd-185-mn-in-1h-2025/)
- Notable rounds:
  - Breadfast: $50m pre-Series C (Mubadala; reported early 2026).
  - Taager: $6.75m (2025).
  - Wuilt: $2m (2025).
  - Mazaya: $5m pre-seed (date unclear).
  - Cartona: $12m Series A (date unclear).
  - TradeHub shut down in September 2025 because it found no product-market fit.
  - Sources: [Shore Africa](https://shore.africa/2026/03/02/breadfast-50m-mubadala-saudi-egypt-ecommerce); [Entrepreneur](https://entrepreneur.com/en-ae/finance/we-got-funded-cairo-based-e-commerce-platform-mazaya/436106); [CairoScene](https://cairoscene.com/Business/Egyptian-B2B-E-Commerce-Platform-Cartona-Raises-12-Million-Series-A); [TechInAfrica](https://www.techinafrica.com/?p=40713)

### Inferences (white space)
- No funded player owns "verified branded off-price fashion, multi-store, one checkout" in Egypt. Investors are funding grocery, B2B and social-commerce enablement, not fashion marketplaces.
- The white space Outlet NK could fill:
  - verified discounts, a trust gap against "up to 50%" event marketing;
  - local brand overstock aggregation, using Instagram brands and store closures as supply;
  - fit and size tools to cut COD refusals and returns.

### Gaps
- Full-year 2025 and 2026 MAGNiTT Egypt e-commerce totals were not found.
