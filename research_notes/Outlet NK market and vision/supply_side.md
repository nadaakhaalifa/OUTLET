# Supply Side of Off-Price Fashion in Egypt and MENA (as of 2026-11-06)

Method note: 12 web searches (English only), no full-page fetches. Most figures come from search-result summaries of news articles. Treat exact numbers as needing a primary check. No Arabic-language sources were searched, which is the biggest gap.

## 1. Egypt's apparel and textile industry: size, export surplus, QIZ and overruns

### Takeaway
Egypt's ready-made garment (RMG) export sector is large and growing fast. Exports were about USD 3.1–3.4bn in 2025, up 20–22%, with QIZ exports to the US at about USD 1.33bn. That makes it a real potential source of overruns and export rejects. However, free-zone and export-oriented output faces customs and tax rules, and tightening GAFI controls, when sold locally. Domestic sale of overruns is legal only after paying duties and taxes, and brand-IP limits are a separate open question.

### Cited Findings
- AECE (export council): garment exports were about USD 3.1bn in Jan–Nov 2025, up 22% y/y, passing USD 3bn for the first time. July 2025 was the peak month at USD 326m. — [Daily News Egypt (Dec 2025)](https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/?p=842885); [MEA TechWatch (Dec 2025)](https://meatechwatch.com/2025/12/30/egypts-apparel-exports-cross-3-billion-mark-for-first-time/)
- Ministry of Investment & Foreign Trade: full-year 2025 RMG exports were USD 3.394bn, up 20% from USD 2.846bn in 2024. This 2024 base conflicts with AECE's ~USD 2.5bn, so the two series differ. — [Zawya/Arab Finance (2026)](https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/egypts-ready-made-garment-exports-rise-20-yoy-in-2025-393134)
- Top markets in 2025 were the US, then the EU, then Türkiye. Egyptian-owned firms made up about 62–63% of export value, rising from USD 1.07bn (2023) to USD 1.60bn (2025). — [Daily News Egypt](https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/?p=841842); [CairoScene (Dec 2025)](https://cairoscene.com/Buzz/Egyptian-Companies-Hold-63-of-Egypt-s-Apparel-Export-Base)
- AECE target: USD 12bn in garment exports by 2031. — [Egyptian Streets (Sep 2025)](https://egyptianstreets.com/2025/09/19/egypts-garment-industry-gains-momentum-with-strong-export-growth/)
- QIZ exports were reported at USD 1.33bn in 2025 versus USD 1.19bn in 2024, with a USD 2bn target for 2026. The source is RMGEC data as reported by Enterprise. Egypt is talking about lowering the Israeli-content requirement in QIZ (Feb 2026). — [Enterprise](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/inside_industry/egyptian-textile-exports-are-up-in-2025-and-tariffs-seem-to-be-helping/); [Enterprise (Feb 2026)](https://enterpriseam.com/logistics/2026/02/26/egypt-eyes-expanding-qiz-membership-as-talks-open-to-reduce-israeli-content-requirement/); [US ITA QIZ page](https://trade.gov/qiz-egypt)
- US apparel exports rose 14% y/y to USD 877m through Aug 2025, and AECE says US tariffs on competitors are helping Egypt. — [Daily News Egypt (2025)](https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/?p=830683)
- ~2,500+ apparel factories and ~1.5m workers. **Unverified**: this comes from a garbled snippet. — [FashionUnited (Nov 2025)](https://fashionunited.com/news/business/could-egypt-be-the-next-garment-producing-hub-by-2035/2025112669353)
- Free-zone garment projects are set up to export 100% of output. GAFI chair (31 Dec 2025): "strict" controls apply. Allowing about 20% of output into the local market duty- and tax-free would be unfair to local firms. — [Al Manassa (Dec 2025)](https://almanassa.com/en/news/29469)
- Goods sold from free zones into Egypt bear a 1% goods-movement fee plus domestic taxes and customs, the same as any other import. — [Andersen Egypt](https://eg.andersen.com/tax-free-zones-egypt/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/withholding-tax/1645034/withholding-tax-in-free-zones-in-egypt)
- The Finance Ministry and GAFI are reportedly considering a 4–5% tax on free-zone products sold locally. **Not confirmed as enacted.** — [Al Manassa](https://almanassa.com/en/news/29469)
- New garment free-zone projects (2026) are approved as 100% export, e.g. one at 40m garments/year. — [Amwal Al Ghad (2026)](https://en.amwalalghad.com/?p=229152)

### Inferences
- Factory overruns and export rejects exist in volume, given about USD 3.4bn of exports, but they mostly sit in free-zone/QIZ factories. Legally they reach local buyers only through customs clearance plus taxes. A HAUL seller of such stock must prove the goods were customs-cleared (release documents).
- Separately from customs, overruns carrying a US/EU brand's label may breach the factory's contract and the brand owner's trademark rights. Under the master prompt's counterfeit and brand-authorization KYC (§19), these listings should require brand authorization or de-labelling. Recommend adding this to the verify register.
- Private-label factories (Egyptian-owned, 63% of exports) are a cleaner supply source if HAUL lists them as unbranded "factory outlet" goods.

### Gaps
- Number of local Egyptian fashion brands, size of the domestic apparel market, and the share made in 10th of Ramadan or Mahalla: no sources found.
- Any data on overrun or export-reject volumes (e.g. typical 2–5% overcut) in Egypt: not found.
- An authoritative legal text on selling QIZ or export overruns domestically, including brand-licence restrictions: not found. **VERIFY with customs counsel.**

## 2. How Egyptian brands and franchise holders liquidate stock today

### Takeaway
The evidence is thin. Visible channels are outlet malls (Tiba Outlet Mall, since 2011), standalone stock outlets, seasonal and Black Friday sales, Instagram-led sales and pop-ups, and Wekalet El Balah for surplus and used goods. I found no sourced data on how the franchise groups (Alshaya, Azadea, Al-Futtaim, Apparel Group, MAF) clear Egyptian stock.

### Cited Findings
- Tiba Outlet Mall (Nasr City) is Egypt's first outlet mall, opened 2011. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiba_Outlet_Mall)
- Older guides list Dress for Less (Tiba), Nile Stock Outlet (Maadi) and Tica's designer outlet (Mohandeseen). — [Cairo360](https://www.cairo360.com/article/shopping/dress-for-less-new-clothes-and-accessories-outlet-in-tiba-mall/)
- Prime Group announced Egypt's "first international outlet mall" (2019). Delivery status unknown. — [Enterprise (2019)](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2019/11/14/prime-group-to-establish-first-international-outlet-mall-in-egypt/?nb=1)
- Wekalet El Balah (Bulaq) is Egypt's largest used-clothing market. Excess stock and lightly used items make up the bulk, and wholesalers import thrifted items and sell them by the kilo. — [The National (2023)](https://www.thenationalnews.com/weekend/2023/04/21/cairos-thriving-used-clothes-market-is-a-sign-of-the-times); [Scoop Empire](https://scoopempire.com/bargains-and-sustainability-on-offer-at-cairos-el-wekala-flea-market-for-clothes/)
- Local brands run heavy November/Black Friday discounting, and Instagram curators (e.g. CairoFinds) aggregate sales. — [Cairo360](https://www.cairo360.com/?p=1744732); [CairoScene](https://cairoscene.com/buzz/cairofinds)
- Cottonil reportedly has 300+ points of sale (third-party profile, unverified). Town Team (Earn Rocket Holding) is expanding to Morocco. — [Yellow Pages EG](https://yellowpages.com.eg/en/brand/cottonil); [Le Desk](https://ledesk.ma/enoff/habillement-legyptien-town-team-arrive-au-maroc-une-unite-de-production-prevue/)

### Inferences
- Liquidation is fragmented and offline: outlet stores, mall sales, stocklot traders and Instagram. That is the gap a curated online off-price channel could fill. This is an inference and has not been validated with sellers.

### Gaps
- Franchise-holder liquidation practice in Egypt (re-export to other markets, jobbers, staff sales): nothing found.
- Stocklot trader volumes and prices, sample-sale and bazaar circuits (e.g. Fashion Bazaar): nothing found in English. Arabic searches and seller interviews are needed.

## 3. Global overstock data and destruction bans

### Takeaway
Estimates of unsold apparel vary widely: 10–40% of 80–150bn garments a year, with an EU-study average of about 21%. The EU ban on destroying unsold apparel and footwear has applied to large companies since 19 July 2026 (medium-sized from 2030). That pushes EU stock toward resale and off-price channels, but its direct effect on Egypt is limited.

### Cited Findings
- 80–150bn garments are made a year, 10–40% are unsold, and no one knows exact production. — [Guardian via PressReader (Jan 2024)](https://www.pressreader.com/usa/the-guardian-usa/20240119/282252375389251)
- Pre-COVID, only ~60% of garments sold at full price. Markdowns averaged 20–30% in 2017. Both are McKinsey figures cited second-hand and **not verified against McKinsey**. — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/how-brands-retailers-and-resellers-are-combating-overproduction); [Raconteur](https://www.raconteur.net/digital-transformation/fashion-unsold-inventory)
- Valuations of 2023 excess stock range from USD 70bn to 140bn, inconsistent across sources (**unreliable**). In an Inventory Planner survey, 44% of US apparel retailers still had unsellable stock after January sales. — [RetailMinded](https://retailminded.com/almost-half-of-us-fashion-stores-are-stuck-with-excess-stock/)
- EU ESPR: the Commission adopted measures on 9 Feb 2026. The ban on destroying unsold apparel, accessories and footwear applied to large companies from 19 Jul 2026 and applies to medium-sized companies from 2030, with SMEs exempt. Large companies must disclose discarded unsold goods immediately. — [European Commission (Feb 2026)](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/new-eu-rules-stop-destruction-unsold-clothes-and-shoes-2026-02-09_en); [EC (Jul 2026)](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/ban-destruction-unsold-clothes-and-shoes-enters-application-2026-07-17_en)

### Inferences
- EU-made but undestroyable stock from brands that produce in Egypt (EU is the #2 export market) may look for outlets outside the EU. It could also return as stocklot supply, but there is no evidence yet.
- Egypt has no equivalent destruction ban that I found.

### Gaps
- No primary McKinsey State of Fashion 2025/2026 figure on inventory was retrieved.
- No Ellen MacArthur figures were retrieved.
- No MENA-specific overstock data was found.

## 4. What brands fear about off-price channels and the controls they want

### Takeaway
Brands fear dilution, price erosion and channel conflict. Some (Puma, Levi's) have cut back on off-price. Successful off-price e-tailers answered with gated access, brand-set pricing and marketing opt-outs. Academic evidence suggests well-positioned outlets can be additive.

### Cited Findings
- Otrium let brands set pricing and merchandising, opt out of marketing that spotlights discounts, and gated the site behind email sign-up. Otrium closed its US operations (Jan 2024) in a deal tied to Simon Property Group, and its UK operations also closed. — [Glossy (2024)](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/luxury/luxury-briefing-why-off-price-fashion-e-tailer-otrium-closed-its-u-s-operations); [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/otrium-fashion-excess-stock)
- Brands worry about shoppers seeing deep discounts in search results. Puma and Levi's are shifting away from off-price to protect brand value. — [The Fashion Law](https://www.thefashionlaw.com/off-price-retailers-faring-well-for-now-as-brands-continue-to-focus-on-distribution/); [PowerCommerce](https://powercommerce.com/blogs/ecommerce-hub/off-price-selling-a-tale-of-two-strategies-as-carter-s-embraces-growth-and-puma-shifts-focus)
- A UT-Dallas study (Management Science) found properly positioned outlet channels bring incremental sales and outlet shoppers later spend at flagships. — [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/study-off-price-stores-actually-help-dont-hurt-flagship-brands/414536)
- Brands such as Under Armour and Ralph Lauren are pulling back from TJ Maxx. — [ABC7](https://abc7ny.com/post/tjmaxx-stores-tj-maxx-marshalls-supply-chain-issues/11232199/)

### Inferences
- Product controls HAUL should offer: members-only or login-gated drops (already 18+ account-required), noindex on brand-event pages, brand-set price floors, a marketing opt-out, and geo-limits.
- A possible tension exists with the §9 Price Integrity Engine, which shows verified "% off" publicly. Brands may want discount depth hidden. A "brand-controlled display" option should still never show unverified claims.

### Gaps
- No Egypt or MENA brand-sentiment survey on off-price was found. Seller interviews are needed.
- Grey and parallel imports in Egypt: no sourced data.

### Takeaway
Seller infrastructure is COD- and logistics-centric. Bosta claims next-day nationwide delivery, Paymob serves hundreds of thousands of merchants and offers embedded Shopify payments. No primary survey of seller priorities (commission, payout speed) was found.

### Cited Findings

- Bosta partnered with e& (Oct 2025) on fintech plus logistics, and with Mastercard (Feb 2026) on SME logistics and payments. — [TechAfrica News](https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/10/mastercard-and-bosta-join-forces-to-empower-egyptian-smes-with-logistics-and-payments-solutions/)
- Paymob merchant counts conflict: 170k (older), 250k (Shopify-launch coverage), and 390k+ across EG/UAE/KSA/Oman as of Sep 2026 (secondary profile). Paymob launched embedded Shopify payments in MENA. — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/paymob-fuels-menas-e-commerce-market-by-offering-embedded-shopify-payments-piwqgo9y); [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments-general/paymob-and-shopify-launch-embedded-payments-in-mena--1269400); [The Paypers/Khazenly](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/paymob-partners-with-khazenly-to-digitalise-egyptian-merchants)

## 6. Counterfeits and the grey market in Egyptian fashion

### Takeaway
Counterfeiting is widespread but poorly quantified. One unverified claim cites 14,000+ counterfeit brands, mainly clothing. Penalties under the IP law are low, and parliament has discussed tougher legislation. HAUL's brand-authorization KYC is therefore essential.

### Cited Findings
- An Egyptian law firm cites a ministry count of 14,000+ counterfeit brands, mostly clothes, cosmetics and FMCG, with fake Nike, Adidas, LV and Gucci imported from Türkiye and sold as originals. **Unverified.** — [Waya Media](https://waya.media/egyptian-law-firm-exposes-operations-of-counterfeit-brands/)
- Trademark counterfeiting carries at least 2 months' prison or a fine of EGP 5,000–20,000. A parliamentary committee adopted tougher draft anti-counterfeit legislation (date unclear). — [Waya Media](https://waya.media/egyptian-law-firm-exposes-operations-of-counterfeit-brands/); [Egypt Independent](https://www.egyptindependent.com/egypts-parliament-adopts-draft-legislation-against-counterfeit-brands/)
- Diesel ran a raid with Egyptian police (undated). — [FashionNetwork](https://ww.fashionnetwork.com/news/Diesel-continues-to-fight-counterfeiting,1180696.html)

### Inferences
- "Export surplus" is a common cover story for counterfeits and grey goods. Listings of global brands should require proof of authorization or customs-cleared purchase, plus pHash image checks (§9.4, §27).

### Gaps
- No 2025–26 Egyptian seizure data or market-size estimate was found.
- Grey-import (parallel) rules for franchise-protected brands in Egypt: not found. **VERIFY** with IP counsel.
