# Global off-price, outlet and flash-sale fashion benchmarks

Research date: 2026-11-06. Years are labelled. "UNVERIFIED" means the source is weak, the figure is a third-party estimate, or it comes from training memory and was not confirmed in this pass. Coverage was limited to about 13 searches, so many items are in Gaps.

## Q1. Scale and economics of the reference players (Veepee, Zalando Lounge, The Outnet, BFL, TJX/Ross/Burlington, Stitch Fix, resale)

### Takeaway
The durable off-price money-makers are store-based buyers with about 30% gross margin and 12% pretax margin (TJX, Ross), plus a few scaled online clubs: Veepee (about €3.3bn in 2024) and Lounge by Zalando (growing about 33%). Pure online luxury off-price has shrunk: The Outnet was sold for about $30m in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **TJX FY2026 (year to 31 Jan 2026):** net sales $60.4bn (+7%), gross margin 31.0% (30.6% in FY25), pretax margin 12.1% (11.7% adjusted), SG&A 19.1%. Lower shrink added 0.2pt to gross margin. Q4 pretax margin was 13.5%. — [TJX 10-K FY2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109198/000010919826000008/tjx-20260131.htm); [TJX Q4 FY26 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/109198/000010919826000004/tjxq4fy26earningspressrele.htm)
- **Ross Stores FY2025 (to 31 Jan 2026):** sales $22.8bn (+8%), comps +5%, operating margin 11.9% (12.2% in FY24). Tariff costs took about 30bp. — [Ross 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/745732/000074573226000006/rost-20260131.htm)
- **Burlington Q1 2025:** adjusted EBIT margin 6.1% on flat comps (secondary source). — [Zacks via TradingView](https://tr.tradingview.com/news/zacks:96b0a0172094b:0-can-burlington-s-margin-strategy-withstand-tariff-pressures)
- **Veepee 2024:** turnover about €3.3bn. It runs time-boxed brand events across about 7,000 brands, and Italy grew twice as fast as Europe. Its 2023 turnover is cited at €3.2bn. — [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/veepee-group-closes-2024-33-billion-italy-flash-sales-grow-twice-as-fast-as-europe-AGPsLRyD); [FashionUnited ES](https://fashionunited.es/noticias/empresas/veepee-cierra-2024-con-una-facturacion-de-3300-millones-de-euros-italia-marca-un-9/2025040845509); [Businesscoot](https://businesscoot.com/en/company/veepee-venteprivee-com)
- **Veepee 2025:** no official figure was found. ECDB estimates GMV of about $3.97bn (UNVERIFIED estimate). — [ECDB](https://ecdb.com/resources/sample-data/retailer/veepee)
- **Veepee Re-turn:** launched in Italy in 2022. Partner brands sell recently returned items through it at a discount, which cuts their returns-handling costs. — [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/veepee-group-closes-2024-33-billion-italy-flash-sales-grow-twice-as-fast-as-europe-AGPsLRyD)
- **Veepee returns policy:** Veepee grew faster than the e-commerce market "despite refusing free shipping and returns". — [RetailDetail](https://www.retaildetail.eu/en/news/fashion/veepee-grows-fast-despite-refusing-free-returns)
- **Zalando:** Lounge by Zalando drove a 33% revenue increase in the Offprice segment in Q1 2025. Group 2025 revenue was about €12.3bn (+~17%) with adjusted EBIT of about €591m. — [Retail Week](https://www.retail-week.com/fashion/zalando-credits-boost-in-revenue-and-improved-profitability-to-online-shopping-club/7043677.article); [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/zalando-says-shares-undervalued-as-ai-b2b-growth-power-2026-outlook-2026-05-15/)

- **ThredUp FY2025:** revenue $310.8m (+20%), reported net of seller payouts, gross margin 79.4%, loss from continuing operations $20.2m, adjusted EBITDA 4.4%. — [ThredUp 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1484778/000148477826000007/tdup-20251231.htm)
- **Vinted 2025:** revenue about €1.1bn (+35–38%, sources conflict), net profit about €62m (−19%), GMV about €10.8bn. Implied revenue/GMV is about 10%. - **Myntra End of Reason Sale (EORS) 2025:**

  - December 2025 (EORS-24): 50–90% off, with 5,000 partner brands taking part for the first time (secondary sources).

### Inferences
- The best-run off-price businesses earn about 30% gross margin and 12% pretax margin, and that comes from buying discipline, fast turns and low shrink, not from markup. A marketplace that takes commission (Outlet NK) carries no inventory risk, so its comparable lever is take rate minus fulfilment, payment, COD and returns costs.
- TJX is putting capital into MENA off-price through BFL. It is a likely future competitor or partner for an Egypt off-price play.
- Event-based clubs (Veepee, Lounge) survive at scale. Single-segment online luxury off-price (The Outnet) did not.
- Veepee shows that paid returns are viable in off-price. This supports Outlet NK's design where the shopper pays change-of-mind return shipping, within CPL limits.

### Gaps
- Veepee EBITDA or profitability, its 2025 revenue, member count and current brand count: no primary source found.
- Zalando's 2025 off-price segment revenue and margin. The annual report was not retrieved.
- Burlington full-year FY2025 results, Nordstrom Rack metrics, and TJX inventory turn: not retrieved.
- Whether BFL operates in Egypt: not confirmed.
- Secretsales and Trendyol's discount model: not researched in this pass.

## Q2. Consignment vs wholesale vs marketplace models, and how brands like to offload stock

### Takeaway
There is little hard 2024–26 data on take rates. The models break down by who holds inventory risk: TJX buys outright, flash clubs mostly buy on consignment or after the event, and marketplaces take commission.

### Cited Findings
- **Zulily:** often described as just-in-time, buying from suppliers only after a sale ended. This kept inventory risk low but made delivery slow. (Secondary source.) — [Failory](https://www.failory.com/cemetery/zulily)
- **Flash-sale dependency:** flash-sale retailers depend on brands to supply them with inventory. — [Mercer Capital](https://mercercapital.com/gone-in-a-flash-ma-impairments-and-flash-sale-websites)
- **Brand-protective channels:** Veepee's Re-turn is a closed channel that lets brands offload returns without public markdowns. — [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/veepee-group-closes-2024-33-billion-italy-flash-sales-grow-twice-as-fast-as-europe-AGPsLRyD)

### Inferences
- Brands choose members-only, time-boxed and closed-door events because these protect list-price perception. For Outlet NK this supports members-only drops, optional geo-restriction, and "secret" (unbranded) sales. Note that an unbranded sale must still show seller legal identity under CPL.

### Gaps
- Typical commission or take rates for off-price marketplaces, and consignment margin splits (Veepee and Zalando Lounge Partner Program): no sourced numbers were found. Collect them from Zalando's Partner Program terms and from seller interviews.

## Q3. Unit economics (CAC, repeat rate, AOV, returns, contribution margin, membership)

### Takeaway
Fashion return rates of about 20–30% for apparel (higher for swimwear and lingerie) erode CAC and margin. Most benchmark figures available are vendor blogs, so treat them as directional.

### Cited Findings
- **Return rates:** apparel about 24–26%, swimwear and lingerie 30–50%. Fashion DTC CAC is cited at about $90–120. Return-adjusted CAC = spend / (new customers × (1 − return rate)). All UNVERIFIED vendor or blog figures. — [Foundry CRO](https://foundrycro.com/blog/dtc-fashion-marketing-benchmarks-2026/); [Rewarx](https://www.rewarx.com/blogs/fashion-ecommerce-returns-profitability-guide)
- **Veepee returns:** a 35% apparel return rate is claimed for 2025. UNVERIFIED (SWOT-template site). — [canvasbusinessmodel](https://canvasbusinessmodel.com/products/veepee-swot-analysis)
- **Zalando:** about one-third of its returns are size-related. — [Zalando](https://corporate.zalando.com/en/node/10416)
- **Stitch Fix:** revenue per client is rising while the client base shrinks. This is the subscription and styling retention risk. — [Stitch Fix 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1576942/000162828025042782/sfix-20250802.htm)

### Inferences
- Model Outlet NK contribution margin per order with an explicit return rate. Use 25% as the base case and 35% as the stress case. Also include COD return-to-origin (RTO) cost.

### Gaps
- No credible public data was found on repeat rate, AOV, contribution margin per order, or the effect of membership in off-price clubs. Veepee membership is free, so it is not comparable to a paid tier. Sources to try next: McKinsey/BoF State of Fashion 2026 and Coresight.

## Q4. Why flash-sale businesses failed, and why the survivors lasted

### Takeaway
US flash-sale sites failed because supply dried up as brands' own outlets and TJX/off-price went digital, because of the cost of acquiring customers, because of slow shipping against Amazon Prime, and because of notification fatigue. The survivors combine scale buying, treasure-hunt rotation, cost discipline and closed brand relationships.

### Cited Findings
- **Gilt:** never reliably profitable. Hudson's Bay Co. bought it in 2016 for about $250m, below the capital it had raised. — [Harvard D3](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-rctom/?p=20444); [Mercer Capital](https://mercercapital.com/gone-in-a-flash-ma-impairments-and-flash-sale-websites)
- **Competition from omnichannel off-price:** Saks OFF 5TH and T.J. Maxx went digital and took flash-sale customers. — [Marketplace](https://marketplace.org/2016/01/07/flashsales); [Fortune 2015](https://www.fortune.com/2015/12/16/flash-sales-trouble)
- **Zulily:**
  - QVC (Liberty Interactive) bought it in 2015 for $2.4bn. Qurate sold it to Regent in May 2023, and it shut down on 22 Dec 2023 through an assignment for the benefit of creditors, not bankruptcy.
  - Cited causes: Amazon competition, which Regent blamed and which led to a price-fixing lawsuit; slow delivery from just-in-time buying; and a move away from treasure-hunt flash sales to everyday low prices.
  - Sources: [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/zulily-goes-out-of-business-liquidate/703373); [Spokesman-Review](https://spokesman.com/stories/2024/mar/06/from-unicorn-to-bust-inside-the-fall-of-seattle-on); [RetailWire](https://retailwire.com/?p=220628); [Failory](https://www.failory.com/cemetery/zulily)
- **Zulily's notification dependence:** cutting push or email frequency reduced revenue accordingly (secondary source). — [Failory](https://www.failory.com/cemetery/zulily)
- **Gilt's Target overselling:** Gilt admitted overselling designer Target goods, an inventory-integrity failure. — [NBC New York](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/local/thread-gilt-oversold-its-target-merch/1898405/)
- **Fab, Amazon MyHabit (closed 2016) and the Rue La La–Gilt combination (2018):** from memory, UNVERIFIED in this pass.

### Inferences
- Lessons for Outlet NK:
  1. Secure a recurring supply pipeline with contracts and channel integrations, not just opportunistic stock.
  2. Delivery speed is decisive; just-in-time post-sale buying is fatal.
  3. Do not make revenue depend on notification spam.
  4. Keep the treasure hunt (rotation, events) rather than moving to everyday low prices.
  5. Oversell incidents destroy trust, which supports the row-per-unit reservation design.

### Gaps
- No primary financial post-mortems exist for Gilt or Fab. Zulily's financials at the end are not public.

## Q5. Evidence that AI fit tools and stylists reduce returns or lift conversion

### Takeaway
Zalando has the best public evidence: size advice cuts size-related returns by about 8–10%. No reliable public conversion-uplift figure was found for AI stylists.

### Cited Findings
- **Zalando 10% figure:** items with size advice have about 10% fewer size-related returns, measured against the same category and season. — [Zalando](https://corporate.zalando.com/en/node/10416); [mind.eu](https://www.mind.eu.com/retail/en/zalando-returns-drop-by-10-thanks-to-sizing-recommendations/)
- **Zalando 2025 figure:** size tools prevented about 8% of size-related returns overall, a more conservative number. A Levi's pilot cut returns by about 40% (company-reported). — [Zalando](https://corporate.zalando.com/en/node/11013); [Returns at Zalando](https://corporate.zalando.com/en/node/10413)
- **Zalando body measurement:** the photo-based body measurement feature (2023) is reported to cut returns by about 10%. — [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/2023/07/zalando-body-measurement-feature-reduces-returns-10)
- **Zalando returns policy:** Zalando tightened its returns policy against excessive returners. — [ecommercegermany](https://ecommercegermany.com/blog/zalando-return-policy-update/)

### Inferences
- Plan for a 5–10% reduction in size-related returns, not in total returns. If about a third of returns are size-related, that is roughly 2–3% fewer total returns.

### Gaps
- Conversion uplift from AI stylists or outfit tools (Amazon, Zalando assistant, Stitch Fix) was not found. Stitch Fix's falling client base suggests AI styling alone does not keep customers.
