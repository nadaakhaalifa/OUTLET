## Current governorates: list, count, recent changes

### Takeaway
Egypt has 27 governorates. Helwan and 6th of October governorates existed from April 2008 and were dissolved in 2011. I found no evidence of any new governorate created in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- 27 governorates today. Helwan and 6th of October were created by presidential decree in April 2008 (split from Cairo and Giza). Luxor was upgraded to a governorate in December 2009. After the January 2011 uprising, Helwan and 6th of October were dissolved back into Cairo and Giza. — [Wikipedia: Administrative divisions of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Egypt)
- ISO 3166-2:EG lists 27 current codes, below. Helwan (EG-HU) and 6th of October (EG-SU) were added in the 30 June 2010 newsletter and later removed. Luxor (EG-LX) was added in 2007. — [Wikipedia: ISO 3166-2:EG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-2:EG)
- A 2014 plan would have added governorates (central Sinai, Alamein/North Coast, a Farafra-Bahariya oases governorate) to reach 28. It was postponed pending "societal dialogue". I found no evidence it was enacted. — [Amwal Al Ghad](https://en.amwalalghad.com/egypt-to-create-3-new-governorates/), [Aswat Masriya](https://www.aswatmasriya.com/en/news/details/13414)
- Search found no 2026 announcement creating a governorate. — WebSearch result summary only. The sources are mostly 2014-2015, so a recent change cannot be excluded.

## Greater Cairo detail

### Takeaway
Cairo governorate is entirely urban and divided into districts (ahya/qisms) in four areas. New Cairo, Fifth Settlement, Tagamoa, Shorouk, Badr, Madinaty and the New Capital all sit in Cairo governorate. Obour is in Qalyubia. Sheikh Zayed and 6th of October City are in Giza.

### Cited Findings
- Cairo is a city governorate, 100% urban. Cairo, Giza and Qalyubia form the "Greater Cairo" planning region, which is not an administrative unit. — [Wikipedia: Administrative divisions of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Egypt)
- Cairo areas and districts per Wikipedia: [Wikipedia: Cairo Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo_Governorate)
  - Northern: Shubra, Zawiya el-Hamra, Hadayek el-Kobba, Rod el-Farag, Sharabiya, Sahel, Zeitoun, Amiriya, Waili.
  - Eastern: Misr el-Gedida (Heliopolis), Nozha, Nasr City East and West, Salam 1 and 2, Matariya, Marg.
  - Western: Manshiyat Nasser, Wasat (Darb el-Ahmar, Gamaliya, Hussein), Boulaq, Gharb el-Qahira (Zamalek, Garden City, Downtown), Abdeen, Azbakiya, Muski, Bab el-Shaaria.
  - Southern: Masr el-Qadima, Khalifa, Mokattam, Basatin, Dar el-Salam, Sayeda Zeinab, Tebbin, Helwan, Maasara, Maadi, Tora, 15th of May City.
  - New cities and districts in Cairo: New Cairo 1, 2 and 3 (First Settlement, Fifth Settlement, Qatamia), Shorouk, Badr, The New Capital. Madinaty is not mentioned on that page.
- Arabic names given by that page's table: Fifth Settlement (New Cairo 1) التجمع الخامس / أول القاهرة الجديدة; First Settlement التجمع الأول / ثان القاهرة الجديدة; Qatamia القطامية / ثالث القاهرة الجديدة; Shorouk الشروق; Maadi المعادي; Mokattam المقطم; Ain Shams عين شمس; 15th May City 15 مايو; Marg المرج; Matareya المطرية; Basatin البساتين; Masara المعصرة.
- The New Capital: in Cairo governorate. Wikipedia says it was officially renamed "The New Capital" on 8 Nov 2025 and became the seat of government in April 2024. Cairo's eastern border was extended by decision to include part of the NAC land (46,000 of 184,000 feddans per Ahram). A Feb 2026 draft law to designate it something (unclear what) was not confirmed. — [Wikipedia: The New Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Capital), [Ahram](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/344205.aspx), [cairo.gov.eg](https://cairo.gov.eg/en/news/news-eng/2022/d7eea44451e54c1493045dc542b15b17)
- Fifth Settlement is a section of New Cairo (qism New Cairo 1), Cairo governorate. New Cairo was incorporated in 2000 with the First and Third Settlements. — [Wikipedia: Fifth Settlement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifth_Settlement)
- Giza governorate: about two dozen markaz/qism divisions, including Giza, Imbaba, Dokki, Sheikh Zayed City, 6th of October City. 6th of October governorate (incl. Bahariya Oasis) merged back in April 2011. — [Wikipedia: Giza Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giza_Governorate)
