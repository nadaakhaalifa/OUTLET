# Virtual outlet mall and immersive shopping (research as of 2026-11-06)

Scope note: these notes come from 7 web searches. Search snippets were mostly secondary or trade blogs, and no pages were fetched in full. Every figure is labelled with its year. "UNVERIFIED" marks a claim from a single or vendor source.

## 1. Virtual stores and 3D malls (Obsess, Emperia, Bicester, Taobao Buy+, Vision Pro, Roblox/Zepeto)

### Takeaway
3D virtual stores produce engagement claims, but there is no independent evidence that they lift conversion at scale. Taobao Buy+ (2016) is the classic failure. Bicester's most durable "virtual" channel is human-assisted shopping through WhatsApp, phone and email with real boutiques, not a 3D village.

### Cited Findings
- Infinite Reality bought Obsess (announced January 2025) and Landvault (July 2024). In April 2025 it claimed more than 150M interactions across them. This is a vendor press release. — [Manila Times/GlobeNewswire 2025](https://manilatimes.net/2025/04/08/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/infinite-reality-surpasses-150-million-interactions-in-immersive-experiences-built-for-brands/2088460)
- Obsess survey (undated): 70% of virtual-store visitors have made a purchase and 60% would again. Obsess also claims client conversion up to 70% higher than e-commerce, AOV +111% at one retailer, and 6–10 min sessions for American Girl. All UNVERIFIED vendor figures with unknown dates. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/obsess-70-of-consumers-who-visit-virtual-stores-have-made-purchases/); [Datainnovation 2021](https://datainnovation.org/2021/08/5qs-for-neha-singh-founder-and-ceo-of-obsess/)
- The Bicester Collection launched a multi-destination "Virtual Village Experience" built with Emperia in November 2023, covering Bicester, La Roca, La Vallée and Fidenza. The stated goal was to extend reach to international audiences. No results were published. — [Mobile Marketing Magazine 2023](https://mobilemarketingmagazine.com/the-bicester-collection-launches-a-multi-destination-virtual-village-experience/)
- Earlier, Value Retail expected about 10% of Bicester Village Shopping Collection sales to come from virtual shopping in a given year. The exact year is unclear; it is likely about 2021 and is UNVERIFIED. — [Yahoo/WWD](https://news.yahoo.com/value-retail-switches-virtual-shopping-040113815.html)
- The current Bicester virtual shopping service lets guests shop with the boutique via WhatsApp, phone or email. It is limited to residents of the village's country. — [The Bicester Collection](https://www.thebicestercollection.com/en/services/virtual-shopping/)
- Taobao Buy+ VR (Singles Day 2016) was reviewed as "underwhelming" and a "strange and lonely experience". Only a tiny fraction of its traffic converted to purchases. — [Vice 2016](https://news.vice.com/story/alibaba-vr-shopping-buy-singles-day); [Harvard D3](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-digit/submission/alibabas-vr-shopping-fad-or-future/index.html)

### Inferences
- For Outlet NK, a 2D "village map" metaphor (zones, store fronts, "event" banners) plus human or WhatsApp concierge is the evidence-backed part of the concept. Full 3D/VR is a novelty with high cost and weak conversion evidence.
- WhatsApp-assisted shopping fits Egypt directly, since the spec already plans a WhatsApp Business inbox (§23).

### Gaps
- Searches returned nothing on Ralph Lauren/Dior virtual-store metrics, Apple Vision Pro shopping-app adoption, Roblox/Zepeto brand outlet sales, or Alibaba's 2024–26 3D shopping.
- No independent audit of Obsess or Emperia figures exists.

## 2. Virtual try-on (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
VTO is mainstream (Google Search/Shopping, Zalando), but no platform has published a clean, controlled return reduction attributable only to VTO. The best figure is ASOS, which reported a 160 bp lower returns rate partly attributed to VTO. Size and fit data remain the proven lever.

### Cited Findings
- Zalando's average return rate is about 50% of items. Zalando says its virtual fitting room produced "significantly fewer returns" but gives no figures. — [Zalando corporate](https://corporate.zalando.com/en/node/10413); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/?p=3628666)
- ASOS reported a 160 bp returns-rate reduction partly attributed to a VTO partnership. The year is unclear and the claim is UNVERIFIED (secondary source). — [Business Report](https://www.businessreport.com/?p=276402)
- Widely quoted "30–40% return reduction" figures come from vendor blogs with no named study. Treat them as UNRELIABLE. — [ai2.work](https://ai2.work/blog/how-ai-virtual-try-on-is-fixing-retail-s-849-billion-returns-crisis)
- Google reportedly folded the Doppl try-on app into Search/Shopping on 30 April 2026. UNVERIFIED (secondary source only). — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/?p=3628666)
- Taobao's AI try-on drew a backlash in 2026 for "the same catalog model with a slightly different jawline". Single vendor blog, UNVERIFIED. — [WeShop AI](https://www.weshop.ai/blog/?p=109902)

### Inferences
- VTO on outlet stock is costly: there are many one-off SKUs, and defect items cannot be shown honestly on generated bodies. Generated try-on images could also hide defects, which conflicts with the spec's honesty rules (§A2.13, CPL). If Outlet NK adds VTO, it should come later, carry an "AI preview" label, and always sit alongside the real defect photos.
- Body photos are P3 data under PDPL, so processing should happen on-device (in line with the spec's §10 Phase 3).

### Gaps
- Searches returned nothing on Myntra, Doji or Snap AR adoption, or Google's VTO usage statistics.

## 3. AI shopping agents and agentic commerce (2025–26)

### Takeaway
Agentic commerce is real but unsettled. OpenAI's ACP/Instant Checkout launched in September 2025, opened to all US users in February 2026, then reportedly retreated to discovery-led in March 2026. Google UCP (January 2026) and Shopify Agentic Storefronts (March 2026) push structured product data. Amazon Rufus claims about $12B incremental annualized sales in 2025. Being agent-ready through clean structured feeds is low-cost and worth doing. Agent checkout is US-only and in flux.

### Cited Findings
- OpenAI Instant Checkout plus ACP (built with Stripe) launched 29 September 2025, with Etsy first and Shopify merchants next. — [SERoundtable 2025](https://www.seroundtable.com/openai-chatgpt-instant-checkout-with-agentic-commerce-protocol-40194.html)
- "Buy in ChatGPT" opened to all US users on 16 February 2026. — [Digital Commerce 360 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/02/16/openai-expands-agentic-commerce-push/)
- In March 2026 OpenAI reportedly pulled back so that merchants run their own checkout, with roughly 12 merchants integrated before the pullback. Sources conflict on the dates and reasons, so this is UNVERIFIED. — [Naturaily](https://naturaily.com/blog/agentic-commerce-shopify-guide); [idukki](https://idukki.io/blog/agentic-checkout-explained-acp-and-instant-checkout)
- Google's Universal Commerce Protocol launched in January 2026 with Walmart, Target and Shopify. Shopify Agentic Storefronts were turned on by default for eligible US stores on 24 March 2026. Secondary sources. — [godberrystudios](https://godberrystudios.com/posts/agentic-commerce-2026-chatgpt-shopify-visa-merchant-playbook/)
- Google agentic checkout (auto-buy on price drop, via AI Mode/Gemini) launched 13 November 2025. Google's Shopping Graph is described as 50B+ listings. — [Fortune 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/11/27/ai-shopping-bots-holiday-gifts-ecommece-amazon-rufus-google-chatgpt-agents/)
- Amazon Rufus was credited with about $12B incremental annualized sales in 2025 (Q4 results). Other sources say about $10B, with 250–300M users; the figures conflict. "Buy for Me" (spring 2025) grew from 65k to 500k+ products by November 2025. Rufus "Auto Buy" on price drop arrived in November 2025. A reported 2026 rebrand to "Alexa for Shopping" is UNVERIFIED. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/amazons-ai-shopping-assistant-drove-12-billion-in-sales-for-2025/); [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/amazon-edges-deeper-agentic-commerce-rufus-auto-buy); [letsdatascience](https://letsdatascience.com/blog/amazon-retires-rufus-alexa-for-shopping-agent)

### Inferences
- Outlet NK should be agent-ready at low cost:
  - schema.org Product/Offer markup on public PDPs;
  - a structured product feed that includes VRP and verified-discount evidence, condition and defect fields, and seller legal identity;
  - a read-only catalog MCP server.
- Agent checkout (ACP/UCP) should be deferred. These protocols are US-centric, unstable, and have no Egyptian PSP support. Delegated payment also raises PCI and CPL questions.
- Price-drop auto-buy (Amazon, Google, November 2025) validates the spec's §28.1. An outlet is a natural fit for it.

### Gaps
- No reporting was found on Perplexity shopping or Klarna's agent metrics, any MENA availability of agent checkout, or ACP's current official status.

## 5. Live shopping (Taobao Live, TikTok Shop, Whatnot, NTWRK, MENA)

### Takeaway
Live commerce is growing fast (TikTok Shop about $64B GMV 2025; Whatnot about $8B live sales 2025). Whatnot shows that live auctions of liquidation and collectible stock work, which is directly relevant to an outlet. There is no evidence that TikTok Shop operates in Egypt.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok Shop's global GMV was about $64.3B in 2025 (+94% YoY, 16 markets) according to Momentum Works. Other estimates are $66B to about $100B. Southeast Asia accounted for $45.6B and the US for $15.1B. Live was 14% of US GMV in 2025, up from 10% in 2024. — [DealStreetAsia 2025](https://cdn.dealstreetasia.com/stories/tiktok-shop-gmv-2025-472662); [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/tiktok-shop-data-analysis/); [KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com/pulses/159225)
- Whatnot:
  - Over $3B GMV in 2024.
  - About $8B live sales in 2025. A conflicting source says about $11B.
  - $265M Series E at a $5B valuation (January 2025).
  - $545M Series G at a $20B valuation (August 2026), with a goal of $15B GMV.
  - [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/whatnot-3-billion-2024-gmv-raises-funding/); [excitingcommerce 2026-01](https://excitingcommerce.de/2026/01/29/qvc-aufgepasst-whatnot-knackt-8-mrd-im-live-shopping/); [excitingcommerce 2026-08](https://excitingcommerce.de/2026/08/07/live-shopping-whatnot-holt-545-mio-bei-20-mrd-bewertung/)
- TikTok and Whatnot both posted record Black Friday/Cyber Monday 2025 live sales. — [Modern Retail 2025](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/marketplace-briefing-tiktok-shop-and-whatnot-notch-record-breaking-black-friday-cyber-monday-sales-as-live-shopping-surges/)
- 77% of MENA consumers discover products on TikTok (TikTok-commissioned study, October 2025). — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2620094)
- Egypt appears only as an "emerging" market on a weak vendor list. No confirmed TikTok Shop launch in Egypt was found. — [awisee](https://awisee.com/blog/tiktok-shop-countries-list)

### Inferences
- Live "outlet hauls" and timed live auctions of defect, sample and overstock lots (Whatnot model) could be a signature Outlet NK feature. Auctions need a ⚠ VERIFY on Egyptian auction and consumer law, honest condition disclosure, and the row-per-unit inventory.
- TikTok would serve as a discovery and traffic channel in Egypt, with checkout on Outlet NK.

### Gaps
- No 2024–26 GMV was found for Taobao Live or NTWRK.
- TikTok Shop's Egypt status is unconfirmed; check TikTok's seller center.
