# Company setup, team and budget for Outlet NK (Egypt, researched 2026-11-06)

Scope note: about 11 web searches. Most sources are secondaries (consultancies, law-firm blogs, salary aggregators, news). Every figure is labelled with its year, and unverified items are flagged. EGP to USD conversions use an assumed rate of about EGP 48–50/USD (2025–26), which is **unverified, so confirm the rate on the day**.

## Company setup (forms, GAFI, registrations, Startup Charter)

### Takeaway
An Egyptian LLC set up through GAFI is quick and cheap: roughly 2–7 business days and low government fees in 2025. VC-backed founders typically add a foreign holding company, but I found no 2025–26 source that quantifies this, so treat it as a gap. The Startup Charter, launched in February 2026, adds a startup classification certificate and a simplified tax regime (Law 6/2025). I found no evidence of a startup "golden license".

### Cited Findings
- **Incorporation time and fees (2025):**
  - GAFI incorporation takes about 7 business days once all documents are filed. A premium service finishes it in 2 business days for an extra EGP 10,000. — [Cliffe Dekker Hofmeyr](https://www.cliffedekkerhofmeyr.com/guides/africa-corporate/egypt/incorporation-process/how-long-does-the-company-incorporation-process-take.html)
  - Another guide says an LLC can be set up at the GAFI Investor Services Center the same day or within a few days. Government cost is about EGP 5,000–15,000+ (≈USD 130–390), excluding professional fees. Full compliance readiness takes 2–4 weeks. Security clearance for foreign managers can take weeks to months. (Consultancy source, unverified.) — [Deel entity setup](https://www.deel.com/blog/entity-setup-egypt/), [Commenda](https://www.commenda.io/?p=1271995)
  - An older all-in consultancy quote put first-year LLC fees at about USD 9,750 (2022). This is a vendor quote. — [Healy draft invoice](https://www.healyconsultants.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/draft-invoice-Egypt.pdf)
- **LLC rules:** an LLC needs at least 2 shareholders and has no statutory minimum capital. Foreigners may own 100%. A single-person company is the lowest-cost form. Budget for stamp tax on contracts and bank transactions. — [Lex Africa](https://lexafrica.com/insights/how-to-start-a-business-in-egypt-a-guide-for-entrepreneurs/), [Healy 2025 entities](https://healyconsultants.com/?p=24447)
- **Digital incorporation:** GAFI has introduced a digital incorporation service. — [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/services/tax/perspectives/digital-service-introduced-for-company-incorporation.html)
- **Tax registrations:**
  - Decree 307/2020 created an e-commerce department at the tax authority. E-commerce businesses must register for income tax within 30 days of starting. — [Chambers TMT 2025 Egypt](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/tmt-2025/egypt), [vatupdate 2024](https://www.vatupdate.com/2024/11/26/egyptian-tax-authority-regulations-and-requirements-for-e-commerce-businesses-2)
  - VAT registration is required at EGP 500,000 in sales. — [vatupdate 2024](https://www.vatupdate.com/2024/11/26/egyptian-tax-authority-regulations-and-requirements-for-e-commerce-businesses-2)
  - VAT is 14%. Law 157/2025 amended the VAT Law but left the rate unchanged (2026 vendor guide). — [Kintsugi 2026](https://trykintsugi.com/sales-tax-guides/africa/egypt.md)
- **Startup Charter (launched February 2026):**
  - The government says it will mobilise about USD 1bn over 5 years. Bridge Middle East reports USD 5bn instead, so the sources conflict. — [Business Today Egypt](https://www.businesstodayegypt.com/Article/1/7117/Egypt-launches-‘Startup-Charter’-to-mobilize-1B-in-funding-over), [Bridge MENA](https://bridgemena.com/news/egypt-launches-the-startup-charter-to-attract-5-billion-in-investments-over-five-years)
  - It brings a unified startup definition, a Startup Classification Certificate, 70+ incentives, a simplified tax regime under Law 6/2025, and a planned licensing guide covering costs and timelines. — [Ahram](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/562024.aspx), [Clyde & Co Feb 2026](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/02/egypt-launches-first-national-startup-charter)
- **Golden license:** GAFI lists a "Golden License" service. It is aimed at strategic or large projects, and I found no link to the Startup Charter. — [GAFI](https://www.gafi.gov.eg/English/MediaCenter/News/Pages/GAFI-Participates-in-the-Launch-of-Egypt%E2%80%99s-First-Startup-Charter.aspx)

### Inferences
- The likely default is an Egyptian LLC as OpCo, with a holding company abroad (Delaware, ADGM or DIFC) added at the first priced round, because investors usually ask for one. Counsel must confirm this.
- Apply for the Startup Classification Certificate early to get Law 6/2025 tax simplification. Check revenue caps.

### Gaps
- No 2025–26 source on holding-company structures (Delaware, ADGM or DIFC) or what they cost. ADGM/DIFC fees are unknown. **VERIFY.**
- No official GAFI fee schedule was found.
- I could not confirm whether a stand-alone e-commerce law, or any registration requirement for one, exists. Chambers 2025 says there is no consolidated e-trade law.

## Licences an e-commerce marketplace needs

### Takeaway
The PDPL executive regulations (Decision 816/2025, in force on 2 November 2025, with compliance by about November 2026) require an annual controller/processor licence. Fees are tiered by record volume, and up to 100k records are reportedly exempt. Direct electronic marketing and cross-border transfers need separate licences. Chambers 2025 reports no general MCIT/ITIDA e-commerce licence; ITIDA handles complaints and e-signature matters.

### Cited Findings
- **PDPL executive regulations:**
  - Decision 816/2025 was adopted on 1 November 2025 and entered into force on 2 November 2025, with a one-year compliance window. — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/egypt-s-pdpl-executive-regulations-issued-one-year-compliance-countdown-begins), [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/36521-executive-regulations-of-the-personal-data-protection-law-decision-no-816-of-2025-including-operational-license-requirement-entered-into-force)
  - Licence fees are annual and tiered by number of records. Up to 100k records is reportedly exempt, and one table lists EGP 200 for 100,001–200,000 records. **Unverified secondary.** — [Tech Hive Advisory](https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-egypts-executive-regulation-for-the-personal-data-protection-law), [Tsaaro](https://tsaaro.com/blogs/egypts-pdpl-is-finally-live-why-november-2026-is-a-hard-stop-for-compliance)
  - Direct electronic marketing needs its own licence, with fees that depend on whether the marketing is for the company itself or for others. A cross-border transfer licence costs 50% of the controller/processor fee. — [Chambers article](https://chambers.com/articles/a-first-look-at-egypt-s-personal-data-protection-executive-regulations), [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/privacy-protection/1768986/the-issuance-of-the-executive-regulations-of-the-data-protection-law-in-egypt-and-the-establishment-of-the-data-protection-centre)
- **E-commerce regulation:** Egypt has no consolidated e-trade law or regulator. ITIDA runs an online complaints system and handles e-signature matters (E-Signature Law 2004). — [Chambers TMT 2025](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/tmt-2025/egypt), [Mondaq e-commerce framework](https://www.mondaq.com/telecoms-mobile-cable-communications/1225322/legal-framework-of-e-commerce-business-in-egypt)
- **Older rule:** sellers had to disclose their tax registration number in online ads. I could not confirm its date or current status. — [Egypt Independent](https://egyptindependent.com/egyptian-cabinet-prepares-bill-to-impose-taxes-on-e-commerce/)

### Inferences
- **Licence checklist:** commercial register, tax card, VAT, ETA e-invoice and e-receipt onboarding, a PDPC controller licence and a DPO, a direct-marketing licence (push/WhatsApp/email marketing), and a transfer licence if any cloud region is outside Egypt.
- The CPA (Consumer Protection Agency) has no licence regime that I found, but CPL 181/2018 obligations apply to every seller and to the platform.
- Payments must go through licensed PSPs (CBE). That belongs to another researcher's scope.

### Gaps
- No primary PDPC fee schedule was found, and the bands above 200k records are unknown. There is a decree-number discrepancy: one source says 81/2025. **VERIFY.**
- DPO qualification requirements and the PDPC licence processing time were not found.
- The status of the ITIDA/MCIT e-commerce registration is unconfirmed.

## Salaries in Egypt, 2025–26

### Takeaway
Local pay data is noisy. Local engineer salaries run from about EGP 100k–250k/yr (Payscale's self-reported base) to the high six figures in EGP for senior hires. Remote USD offers ($1.5k–3k/month and up, with remote medians of $60–70k/yr) set the competitive ceiling for good engineers and PMs.

### Cited Findings
- **Payscale software engineer averages (2025, annual base, self-reported):**
  - React: EGP 101,736 overall and about EGP 120,000 in Cairo.
  - SQL: EGP 150,024.
  - Senior with SQL: EGP 244,168.
  - Early-career .NET: EGP 108,000.
  - Sources: [Payscale React](https://www.payscale.com/research/EG/Job=Software_Engineer/Salary/30ec86b5/React.js), [Payscale Senior SQL](https://www.payscale.com/research/EG/Job=Senior_Software_Engineer/Salary/f71f4851/SQL)
  - Payscale's bottom-percentile figures are implausible, so treat the dataset as a floor.
- **Junior engineers in Cairo (2025):** about EGP 7k–12k/month. A remote USD contract of $1.5k–3k/month beats many local offers. — [Sprints.ai 2025](https://sprints.ai/ar-eg/blog/programmer-salaries-in-egypt-2025)
- **Product manager (sources diverge widely):**
  - Local estimates: ILO-based figure of about EGP 948,500/yr gross (2025) — [Life Indexed](https://lifeindexed.com/salary/africa/egypt/product-manager/2025/); TalentUp Cairo median EGP 733,900 from n=38, May 2025 — [TalentUp](https://talentup.io/salary/product%20manager/cairo); Payscale about EGP 150–180k base — [Payscale PM](https://www.payscale.com/research/EG/Job=Product_Manager%2C_Software/Salary).
  - Remote PM median: about USD 59,800/yr base. — [Plane](https://plane.com/salaries/product-manager/egypt)
- **Remote engineers:** Plane gives a remote DevOps median of about USD 69,078. This is vendor data and internally inconsistent. — [Plane DevOps](https://plane.com/salaries/devops-developer/egypt)
- **Talent competition:**
  - In December 2025, Egypt was described as "one of the most contested talent pools" in global tech. — [Ecofin](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2212-51601-egypt-emerges-as-a-one-of-the-global-battleground-for-tech-engineers-and-industrial-talent)
  - GCC and global employers poach Egyptian staff and founders move to KSA and the UAE. This is an older article. — [Entrepreneur ME](https://mena.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/the-impact-of-egypts-currency-devaluation-on-its-local/476269)
  - 2025 hiring trends overview. — [Qureos](https://qureos.com/hiring-guide/hiring-trends-in-egypt)

### Inferences (planning ranges, NOT sourced; validate with Wuzzuf, Glassdoor and recruiters)

  - Mid-level engineer: EGP 40–80k.
  - Engineering manager or CTO: EGP 150–300k+, or USD 4–8k if paid in USD.
  - Product manager: EGP 60–120k.
  - UX designer: EGP 35–80k.
  - Data or ML engineer: EGP 70–150k.
  - QA: EGP 25–60k.
  - DevOps: EGP 70–140k.
  - Operations manager: EGP 40–90k.
  - Customer support agent: EGP 9–15k.
  - Partnerships manager: EGP 40–80k.
  - Marketing manager: EGP 50–110k.
- These are informed extrapolations from the data above, not sourced figures.
- Add employer social insurance (about 18.75% of the insured wage, up to a cap; **VERIFY**) plus medical insurance.

## Funding landscape

### Takeaway
Egyptian startups raised about USD 614m in 2025 including debt, according to the Ministry of Planning. Equity only, Magnitt reports USD 202m for the first nine months, down 37% year on year. E-commerce took about 24% of equity funding. Disclosed commerce seed rounds have a median of about USD 2.6m (small sample). ITIDA programs offer grants worth tens of thousands of dollars.

## Operating costs, burn and runway

### Takeaway
Warehouse asking rents in Greater Cairo (2025) range from about EGP 65–400 per sqm per month. I found no sourced data on coworking, photo studios, marketing budgets or seed-stage burn.

### Cited Findings
- **Warehouse asking rents (2025 listings):**
  - Abou Rawash: EGP 250/sqm, 2,475 sqm for EGP 618,750/month. — [Property Finder](https://www.propertyfinder.eg/en/plp/commercial-rent/warehouse-for-rent-cairo-abou-rawash-7702664.html)
  - Obour: about EGP 300–350/sqm (590 sqm for EGP 177k/month), and a 2,400 sqm unit for EGP 160k/month (about EGP 67/sqm).
  - New Cairo industrial zone: 400 sqm for EGP 150k/month (about EGP 375/sqm).
  - Source for these: [Bayut](https://www.bayut.eg/en/property/details-502072123.html)
  - These are asking prices, not transaction prices.

### Inferences (unsourced planning assumptions; VERIFY)
- **Hub:** a 300–500 sqm starter hub would cost about EGP 75–150k/month in rent before fit-out, at 2025 asking rates.
- **Launch cost model:** a seed marketplace with a team of about 20–30 in Egypt plausibly burns USD 80–200k/month. USD 2–3m would then give about 18–24 months of runway. This is my own estimate.

### Gaps
- Coworking prices in Cairo (for example Greek Campus, Regus, Spaces) were not found.
- Photography studio costs were not found.
- I found no sourced benchmarks for performance-marketing budgets at Egyptian launches, or CAC.
- I found no sourced burn or runway figures for Egyptian seed-stage e-commerce.
