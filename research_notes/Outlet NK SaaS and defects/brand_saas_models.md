# Brand-side SaaS and B2B models for liquidating excess, end-of-season and defective fashion inventory

Research date: 2026-11-06. Most vendor rate cards are private and contract-negotiated. Figures below come mostly from third-party sources and are flagged UNVERIFIED unless a primary source is cited. Search-only pass (11 queries, no deep fetches), so treat this as a starting map.

## How do outlet, resale, B2B and liquidation platforms monetize brands (tiers, commission, onboarding, analytics)?

### Takeaway
The dominant model is commission or revenue share on sold units, often with zero upfront fees. Subscriptions appear mainly as small fixed platform fees (Zalando ~EUR 40/month, B-Stock Supply USD 99/month). White-label resale vendors (Trove, Archive, ThredUp RaaS) negotiate custom deals, and ThredUp moved to no upfront and no monthly fees in 2025. Publicly verifiable commission percentages are scarce.

### Cited Findings
- **Otrium (NL off-price marketplace):** works on a revenue-share basis, splitting sales with brands. Otrium holds the inventory, sets up the brand storefront and handles storage, sales and shipping. Brands keep control of pricing, merchandising and visibility, and get access to sales data. No public split % was found. — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/luxury/luxury-briefing-why-off-price-fashion-e-tailer-otrium-closed-its-u-s-operations); [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/otrium-raises-102-3m/); [Fibre2Fashion](https://fibre2fashion.com/news/e-commerce-industry/otrium-raises-24-mn-to-extend-fashion-s-life-cycle-267565-newsdetails.htm)
- Otrium closed its US operations (date not confirmed in this pass). This is a caution on the economics of off-price expansion. — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/luxury/luxury-briefing-why-off-price-fashion-e-tailer-otrium-closed-its-u-s-operations)
- Otrium affiliate commission is up to 10% with a 30-day cookie (year unknown). This is a publisher fee, not a brand fee. — [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/otrium-uk)
- **ThredUp RaaS (2025):** removed the upfront and monthly fees for branded resale shops and lowered the usage-based fees for Clean Out and cash-out. The pitch is "no tech build, no upfront cost, no monthly fees", with bulk consignment or drop-ship via API. — [ThredUp newsroom](https://newsroom.thredup.com/news/thredup-raas); [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/thredup-overhauls-resale-service-slashes-fees-brand-recommerce/747934/); [ThredUp client portal](https://client-portal.thredup.com/)
- In 2024 ThredUp tested a premium service with a higher processing fee. — [Modern Retail, Jun 2024](https://www.modernretail.co/operations/thredup-is-testing-out-a-new-premium-service-with-a-higher-processing-fee/)
- **Trove / Recurate:** white-label resale where the brand keeps customer data and pricing. Trove is paid through processing-volume fees, platform licensing or revenue share, and terms are not public. Trove acquired Recurate in Aug 2024 as a lighter product for smaller brands. — [yespress (secondary)](https://yespress.io/trove); [FashionUnited, Aug 2024](https://fashionunited.uk/news/business/recommerce-tech-provider-trove-acquires-resale-marketplace-recurate/2024081377085)
- **Treet:** covers DTC peer-to-peer resale, trade-in, **off-price sales and return rerouting**. No pricing found. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/recurate-vs-treet-1)
- **Archive:** no pricing found.
- **B-Stock (B2B liquidation auctions):** charges a commission on sold value that varies by volume (2018 source). There are no upfront fees, and an enterprise seller gets a branded private marketplace in ~30 days. B-Stock collects payment on the seller's behalf and buyers pay a buyer's premium. Small and mid-size sellers on **B-Stock Supply** pay a **USD 99/month subscription plus a USD 50 minimum fee for auctions closing under USD 500** (third-party, UNVERIFIED, ~2026). Generic liquidator commissions run 10–25% of the sale price (non-B-Stock source). — [Digital Commerce 360, 2018](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2018/07/06/a-b2b-liquidation-portal-provider-gets-65-million/); [B-Stock enterprise](https://bstock.com/sellers/enterprise); [B-Stock payments blog](https://bstock.com/blog/how-does-the-payment-process-work/); [entreresource](https://entreresource.com/best-liquidation-sites/); [Maas Companies](https://maascompanies.com/blog/article/liquidation-fees-for-sellers)
- **Faire (B2B wholesale):** charges 15% on reorders. In 2023 Faire said US/CA opening-order commission dropped from 25% to 15% plus a $10 new-customer fee. Many third-party guides still cite 25% first order / 15% repeat (conflict, so verify). Retailers the brand refers itself are commission-free. Payment processing costs 1.9–3.5% depending on payout terms, and international first orders reportedly cost ~35% (UNVERIFIED). — [Faire blog, 2023](https://blog.faire.com/selling/north-america-pricing-update/); [Craftybase](https://api.craftybase.com/blog/how-much-does-faire-charge)
- **Zalando Partner Program:** commission is typically 5–25% by category and price, charged only on non-returned sales. One trade report cites a **EUR 40/month fee and commissions up to 22%** (7–25% for exclusives), year unconfirmed. Exact rates are confidential per contract (UNVERIFIED). — [ecommercenews.eu](https://ecommercenews.eu/?p=30766); [Koongo](https://www.koongo.com/?p=5340)
- **Lounge by Zalando:** a closed, members-only club positioned as a customer-acquisition entry point for brands. 55% of Lounge customers also shop full price on Zalando. — [Zalando corporate](https://corporate.zalando.com/en/node/10813)
- **Veepee:** historically buys surplus and past-season stock at discount for 3–5-day flash sales. It consolidates orders, receives the bulk shipment from the brand and ships parcels, and a buying committee vets brands strictly (secondary sources). — [Veepee pressroom history](https://www.pressroom.veepee.com/history); [Tradebyte](https://www.tradebyte.com/en/channels/veepee/)
- **Joor, Shopify Markets, Liquidity Services:** no fee data was retrieved in this pass.

### Inferences
- Brands accept outlet and off-price channels when they get control over price, visibility and brand image (Otrium, Lounge closed club). This supports §9 price integrity and a "brand-controlled discount visibility" tool as a sellable feature.
- The market trend (ThredUp 2025, B-Stock enterprise, Otrium) is **no upfront fees with monetization on sell-through**. A high subscription fee would be a barrier for Egyptian brands. A low fixed fee (Zalando EUR 40, B-Stock USD 99) plus commission is the common hybrid.
- B-Stock shows a separate **B2B stocklot channel** (auctions to resellers, buyer's premium, branded private portal) that suits defective and bulk lots that should not reach consumers.

### Gaps
- Otrium split %, Archive/Trove/Treet/Recurate prices, Joor subscription, Liquidity Services and Boxed Liquidation fees, and Veepee brand-services fees were not found (private contracts).
- No primary B-Stock price page was fetched.

## What tools do brands pay for (sync, AI listing, markdown optimization, analytics, white-label, B2B)?

### Takeaway
Markdown and inventory optimization is an enterprise category with vendor-claimed gains of a few points of margin. Sync, white-label storefronts and B2B portals are usually bundled into commission deals rather than sold separately.

### Cited Findings
- **Invent Analytics markdown optimization:** at a European apparel retailer with 400+ stores, seasonal revenue rose 2.4% and markdown loss fell 2%, with a claimed 30x ROI. The 6.9% figure appears as "revenue in the markdown period" in one source and as "sell-through" in another. More than 30 clients report a 2–6% profitability increase (vendor claim). — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/invent-analytics/case-study/a-major-european-apparel-retailer-improves-its-revenue-by-24-and-lowers-markdown-loss-by-2); [vendor PDF](https://cdn.featuredcustomers.com/CustomerCaseStudy.document/invent-analytics_major-european-apparel-retailer_876469.pdf)
- **Mavi (Turkish denim, regional relevance):** reported 9.6% revenue growth and 10.6% gross margin growth from Invent's bundle of allocation, replenishment, markdown and transfer tools. — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/invent-analytics/case-study/iconic-fashion-retailer-mavi-achieves-96-revenue-growth-with-invent-analytics)
- **Onebeat:** Crystal cut stock-outs 70% and inventory 50% in 2.5 months (vendor case). Claims of up to 20% higher full-price sell-through and up to 33% less inventory come from a reseller page (UNVERIFIED). Onebeat raised an additional USD 15m in May 2025. — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/onebeat/case-study/crystal-customer-case-study); [VCA Online, 2025](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2025050609/onebeat-raises-additional-15m-to-rewrite-the-rules-of-retail--led-by-schooner-capital/)
- ThredUp bundles a managed storefront and API consignment, and B-Stock bundles a branded private portal. Both are included in commission or fee deals rather than sold as separate SaaS. — sources above.

### Inferences
- Outlet NK can sell a lightweight SMB "markdown advisor" (already §19.1) at a premium tier. The realistic value claim is low-single-digit margin points, not dramatic gains.
- The tools most likely to justify a subscription in Egypt are Shopify/Woo sync, AI Arabic listing generation, a defect-grading listing flow and analytics. These match spec §19.

### Gaps
- No pricing found for Revionics, Crisp, Invent or Onebeat (enterprise quote-only).

## Hybrid marketplace + SaaS for SMB sellers in emerging markets (Shopify Egypt, Wuilt, Zid, Salla)

### Takeaway
MENA store-builders are converging on free or low entry tiers. Wuilt pivoted to free in 2025, and Salla has a free plan with only payment-gateway fees. This sets an Egyptian SMB price anchor near zero for software, so commission-led pricing is safer.

### Cited Findings
- **Wuilt (Egypt):** dropped monthly merchant fees, a business-model shift, and expanded a free store builder across MENA after raising USD 2m (2025). An older listing shows a Basic plan at USD 99/year (likely pre-pivot, UNVERIFIED). — [TechAfrica News, Aug 2025](https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/04/cairos-wuilt-raises-2-million-to-fuel-e-commerce-expansion-after-bold-pivot/); [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/wuilt/pricing)
- **Salla (KSA):** free plan with transaction fees waived (gateway fees only). Basic is ~AED 115/month and Plus ~AED 230/month per one aggregator, while another estimates Plus at USD 49/month. These conflict and are UNVERIFIED. — [storestarter](https://storestarter.pages.dev/ae-en/ecommerce-platform-middle-east/salla-vs-zid); [softwares.com](https://www.softwares.com/software/salla/pricing)
- **Zid (KSA):** has no free plan. Growth is ~AED 230/month (UNVERIFIED). Zid pays partners up to 35% commission on subscriptions referred, and a new shipping pricing structure started 1 Jan 2025. — [storestarter](https://storestarter.pages.dev/ae-en/ecommerce-platform-middle-east/salla-vs-zid); [Zid channel](https://channel.zid.sa/en); [Zid help](https://help-partner.zid.sa/en/articles/10325692-critical-update-new-shipping-pricing-structure)
- **Shopify Egypt EGP pricing:** not found in this pass.

### Inferences
- Suggested Outlet NK structure, as a hypothesis for an ADR: a Free tier at a higher commission, and a paid tier with a modest monthly fee, lower commission and advanced tools (sync, AI listings, markdown advisor, B2B stocklot portal). This mirrors the Zalando and B-Stock Supply hybrids. Commission and pricing are irreversible decisions under A4 and need owner approval.
- Zid's 35% partner revenue share suggests agency or referral channels are a viable acquisition lever in MENA.

### Gaps
- Official Salla, Zid and Shopify EGP price pages were not fetched. Verify directly.

## Unit economics and brand payback from liquidation platforms

### Takeaway
No credible public payback or ROI data exists for brands using Otrium, ThredUp RaaS or B-Stock. The only quantified figures are vendor-claimed markdown-optimization gains.

### Cited Findings
- Invent claims a 30x ROI and a 2% reduction in markdown loss (vendor). — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/invent-analytics/case-study/a-major-european-apparel-retailer-improves-its-revenue-by-24-and-lowers-markdown-loss-by-2)
- Lounge by Zalando's acquisition value: 55% of its customers also buy full price on Zalando. This is an indirect benefit to brands. — [Zalando corporate](https://corporate.zalando.com/en/node/10813)
- Faire guidance says brands price 10–15% higher on the platform to absorb commission (third-party). — [Craftybase](https://api.craftybase.com/blog/how-much-does-faire-charge)

### Inferences
- Brand-side value should be modelled as recovery rate (net proceeds ÷ cost or RRP) versus alternatives such as jobbers, stocklot buyers and destruction. Egyptian stocklot recovery benchmarks still need primary interviews.

### Gaps
- No Egypt or MENA data on stocklot recovery rates, Otrium net-to-brand %, or brand payback periods. Needs seller interviews.
