# AI & automation end-to-end for an off-price marketplace (Outlet NK)

Research date: 2026-11-06. Note that almost all impact figures found are company- or vendor-reported. Independent, audited evidence is rare and is flagged below.

## AI listing from photos (attributes, bilingual copy, background removal, on-model imagery)

### Takeaway
Gen-AI listing and imagery tools have proven adoption at scale (Amazon, Zalando), and the evidence of time and cost savings is strong, though company-reported. I found no independent evidence that they lift conversion.

### Cited Findings
- (May 2025, Amazon-reported) More than 900,000 sellers had used Amazon's gen-AI listing tools, and sellers accepted the generated content with little or no editing about 90% of the time. Amazon also launched "Enhance My Listing", which suggests titles, attributes and descriptions that the seller accepts, rejects or edits — [EcommerceBytes](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/2025/05/08/amazon-adds-more-gen-ai-capabilities-for-sellers/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/08/amazons-newest-ai-tool-is-designed-to-enhance-product-listings)
- (2025 data, reported Apr 2026, Amazon-reported) More than 12 million listings were created with Amazon's AI listing tools in 2025, and the Seller Assistant had more than 230k monthly users — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/amazon/2026/amazon-sellers-gain-sales-and-cut-costs-with-ai-tools/)
- (2025, Zalando via Reuters, company-reported) Gen-AI cut image production from 6–8 weeks to 3–4 days and cut costs by about 90%. About 70% of editorial campaign images in Q4 2024 were AI-generated. Zalando is also building "digital twins" of real models. Zalando's VP of content solutions said AI content is "not better" than human-made content, only faster and more relevant — [The Star/Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/05/07/zalando-uses-ai-to-speed-up-marketing-campaigns-cut-costs)

### Inferences
- The safe pattern is a copilot: AI suggests the Arabic and English titles and attributes, and the seller approves them, as Amazon does (and as the master prompt §19 already requires). The 90% acceptance rate suggests most sellers will need few edits.
- On-model imagery generated by AI should be labeled as such, because the CPL 181/2018 no-misleading rule applies. For defects and condition on outlet stock, real photos must stay mandatory.

### Gaps
- I did not verify measured impact for Shopify Magic, Botika or Lalaland.ai. Their claims are vendor-only.
- I found no published quality evaluation of AI-generated Arabic product copy.

## Dynamic markdown / clearance pricing

### Takeaway
Markdown optimization based on elasticity is a mature category. Vendor case studies consistently report sell-through gains of about 6–10% and margin gains of 4–6%, but all of them come from vendors. McKinsey is the closest to an independent source.

### Cited Findings
- (Vendor) A European apparel retailer with about 400 stores got +6.9% sell-through and 2% lower markdown loss in an A/B cross-matrix test, using Invent.ai — [invent.ai](https://www.invent.ai/case-study/markdown-optimization-solutions-case-study)
- (Vendor) A global footwear and sports-apparel retailer got +10% sell-through and +6% gross margin in one year, using Antuit — [Antuit](https://www.antuit.ai/resources/case-study-0/global-footwear-sports-apparel-retailer-improves-sell-through-and-recaptures-lost-margins)
- (Vendor) NYDJ got 2.6× faster stock rotation, sold 7% more inventory and halved its pricing-ops time, using Pricen — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/pricen/case-study/nydj-uses-ai-pricing-to-drive-markdowns-and-progressive-optimization)
- (Vendor) A designer brand got +71% rate of sale and about +6% margin, using Increff — [Increff](https://www.increff.com/case-studies/how-increff-helped-a-designer-brand-improve-ros-by-71-with-markdown-optimization)
- (McKinsey, undated PDF) A pilot in about 50 stores improved markdown margin by 4–5% in one year — [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/McKinsey/Industries/Retail/Our%20Insights/Pairing%20advanced%20analytics%20with%20intuitive%20tools%20to%20transform%20retail%20markdown%20management/Pairing-advanced-analytics-with-intuitive-tools-to-transform-retail-markdown-management.pdf)

### Inferences
- For Outlet NK this should be advice to sellers (master prompt §19.1), not automatic repricing. Any new price still shows "% off" only if the Price Integrity Engine verifies it. A price ladder fits the published-schedule "Price ladder" drop type in the master prompt.

### Gaps
- I did not find how TJX, Ross, Veepee or Zalando Lounge set prices algorithmically; they do not disclose it publicly.
- I found no MENA elasticity data.

## Visual search, shop the look, virtual try-on

### Takeaway
Virtual try-on (VTO) is now mainstream: Google launched it in the US in 2025 and in the UK and India in December 2025. However, claims that it lifts conversion or cuts returns are almost all vendor marketing with conflicting numbers. Visual-search statistics are old and commissioned by Pinterest.

### Cited Findings
- (2025) Google VTO began in Search Labs in May 2025 and launched fully in the US in July 2025. It uses a full-length photo of the shopper and covers shirts, pants, skirts and dresses — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/20/google-adds-ai-powered-shopping-features-for-discovery-and-easy-check-out); [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/ai/shopping-with-google-virtual-ai-try-on-search-price-alerts-updates)
- (Dec 2025) Google VTO rolled out in the UK and India; it is not available in Egypt — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/fashion/google-launches-virtual-apparel-try-on-tool-in-the-uk-and-india/2025120385179)
- (Vendor aggregators, 2025–26, UNVERIFIED) VTO return reductions claimed at 15–40% and conversion lifts at 20–35%, with some pages claiming 64–94%. A "McKinsey 14 brands" figure is cited, but I could not trace it to a primary source — [Morphed](https://morphed.app/stats/virtual-try-on-statistics); [Fynd](https://www.fynd.com/blog/virtual-try-on-technology-for-ecommerce-a-retailer-s-guide)
- (2018–2020, Pinterest-commissioned) Lens reached 600M visual searches per month by Feb 2018. In a GroupM study, 80% of Pinterest users said they start shopping with visual search, vs 58% of non-users — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/pinterest-lens-sees-600-million-visual-searches-every-month); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/pinterest-announces-lens-updates-releases-new-stats-on-visual-search/563115/)

### Inferences
- Build priority: visual "similar items" search comes first, because it reuses the CLIP/SigLIP embeddings already planned for price matching. VTO is Phase 3, needs a DPIA because body photos are P3 data, and should be measured with an A/B test on returns, not on vendor claims.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve any independent RCT on whether VTO reduces returns.
- I did not get Doji or Zalando VTO outcome metrics.

## Fraud and quality (counterfeits, return fraud, COD risk)

### Takeaway
Amazon has proactive counterfeit controls at scale. Entrupy's accuracy claims (99%+) are its own and are limited to luxury goods. COD/RTO risk scoring is a standard pattern, but the MENA evidence comes only from vendors.

### Cited Findings
- (2024, Amazon-reported) Amazon seized more than 15M counterfeit products, and proactive controls blocked over 99% of suspected infringing listings before brands reported them. Project Zero lets brands remove listings themselves. Sources disagree on how many brands are enrolled: 35k vs 22k — [About Amazon](https://aboutamazon.com/news/policy-new-views/amazon-brand-protection-report-2024-counterfeit-products); [Riverbend](https://riverbendconsulting.com/blog/amazon-project-zero/)
- (Vendor) Entrupy claims 99.1% accuracy on some pages and 99.86% on others, trained on 90M+ images. It covers luxury bags and leather goods, and for sneakers its accuracy is guaranteed only on new items. It offers a 1-year financial guarantee. I found no independent audit — [Entrupy FAQ](https://www.entrupy.com/faq-luxury-authentication/)
- (2026, vendor) COD is the dominant payment method in Egypt. eGrow describes rules-based RTO risk points with pre-dispatch verification for medium-risk orders — [eGrow](https://www.egrow.com/en/blog/auto-flag-fraud-pattern-cod-2026); [eGrow software](https://www.egrow.com/en/blog/best-cod-software-egypt-2026)
- (India, vendor) Cashfree and Shadowfax use ML RTO models (pincode history, customer history, category) that can disable COD per customer. The claim of "up to 40% RTO reduction" is unverified — [Cashfree](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/payments/checkout/cod/rto.md); [Shadowfax](https://shadowfax-admintest.shadowfax.in/blogs/ai-helps-reduce-rto-in-ecommerce-logistics)

### Inferences
- Outlet NK should start with rules plus GBDT and use courier outcome data as labels, consistent with master prompt §20.7. Entrupy-style authentication is relevant only if luxury categories are listed; for mass outlet stock, brand authorization documents and cross-store pHash checks matter more.

### Gaps
- I found no independent COD refusal or fraud rates for Egypt.
- I did not research return-fraud ML evidence such as Appriss Retail.

## Arabic LLM capabilities (MSA, Egyptian dialect, Franco-Arabic)

### Takeaway
Egyptian dialect and Arabizi (Latin-script Arabic) remain measurably harder than MSA. Specialized models such as Nile-Chat beat general open models on Egyptian benchmarks, and dialect benchmarks now exist to evaluate vendors.

### Cited Findings
- (2025) Nile-Chat (4B, 3×4B-A6B MoE, 12B) handles Egyptian Arabic in both Arabic and Latin script. It beats LLaMA, Jais and ALLaM on new Egyptian benchmarks, and the 12B model scores +14.4% over Qwen2.5-14B on the Latin-script benchmarks. The models are open — [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2025.arabicnlp-main.25)
- (COLING 2025) AraDiCE found that Arabic-specific models (Jais, AceGPT) beat multilingual models on dialect tasks, but dialect identification, generation and translation remain hard — [AraDiCE](https://preview.aclanthology.org/setup/2025.coling-main.283)
- (Oct 2025) DialectalArabicMMLU has about 22K QA pairs across 5 dialects, including Egyptian — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.27543v1)

### Inferences
- Before choosing a stylist, search or support LLM, run an Egyptian dialect and Franco-Arabic golden set (master prompt §T12). An open Egyptian model such as Nile-Chat could be self-hosted in-region, which would avoid the PDPL cross-border transfer issue.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve 2026 scores for frontier models (Claude, GPT, Gemini) on Egyptian dialect.
- I found no data on resolution rates for Arabic support chatbots.

## Sustainability: textile waste in Egypt and "saved from landfill" metrics

### Takeaway
The best-supported Egyptian figure is about 212k tonnes a year of pre-consumer textile waste (2019–2021 survey). Resale platforms' impact numbers depend heavily on displacement-rate assumptions (about 60–80%), which critics call soft.

### Cited Findings
- (2021 survey of 46 firms; also dated to 2019) Egypt produces about 212,000 t per year of pre-consumer textile waste, of which 80k t is 100% cotton and 47% is mixed fibres — [SwitchMed](https://switchmed.eu/news/egypt-potential-textile-waste-mapping/); [UNIDO PDF](https://hub.unido.org/sites/default/files/publications/SwitchMed%20Business%20case%20Investing%20in%20advanced%20recycling%20technologies%20for%20fiber-to-fiber%20production%20in%20Egypt_EN.pdf)
- (2016 World Bank, via Ensia 2024) Textiles make up as much as 1.4M t of Egypt's municipal waste per year; the scope is unclear — [Ensia](https://ensia.com/articles/sustainable-fashion-egypt-economy-upcycle-recycle-environment-landfills-waste/)
- (Mar 2026) An EGP 600M recycling plant is planned, producing 30k t per year of felt — [Textile Insights](https://textileinsights.in/?p=15874)
- (ThredUp methodology) ThredUp's calculator uses a Green Story LCA and assumes a secondhand item replaces a new purchase 72% of the time; category rates range from 70% to 80% — [ThredUp](https://www.thredup.com/bg/p/fashion-footprint-sources); [Kurb](https://kurbonline.substack.com/p/how-sustainable-are-fashion-resellers)
- (2025) WRAP's average displacement rate is above 60% for resale and above 80% for repair — [Resource Media](https://resourcemedia.eco/article/wrap-develops-method-retailers-calculate-footprint-gain-textile-repair-and-resale)

### Inferences
- Outlet NK should show only a counted metric ("units of clearance stock sold") and avoid tonnes or CO2 claims unless it publishes a methodology page using a conservative displacement rate (WRAP's 60%). This matches master prompt §28.9 and §T10.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable figure for post-consumer clothing waste or for deadstock/overstock in Egypt.
