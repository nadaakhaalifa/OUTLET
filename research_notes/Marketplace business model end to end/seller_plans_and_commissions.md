# Seller plans, commissions and fees: fashion marketplaces, MENA focus

Scope note: research was limited (about 8 searches, 1 failed fetch; official fee pages were mostly not retrievable). Most figures come from third-party guides and are LOW to MEDIUM confidence. No FX conversion was done because few prices were in local currency; where USD only, EGP not computed (state a rate yourself when needed). Not a price recommendation.

## 1. What do five-tier seller ladders look like, and what is gated by tier?

### Takeaway
Only Shopify (Basic/Grow/Advanced/Plus, plus Starter) and eBay Stores (several store tiers) and Etsy (free vs Plus) showed paid ladders; none of the MENA fashion marketplaces surveyed showed a published subscription ladder, they are commission-led. I found no verified five-tier ladder in MENA.

### Cited Findings
- Shopify 2026 list prices (USD/month): Basic $39 ($29 annual), Grow $105 ($79 annual), Advanced $399 ($299 annual), Plus from about $2,300 (sources differ, $2,300 on 3-yr term or $2,500 on 1-yr). Third-party gateway fee if not using Shopify Payments: 2% / 1% / 0.6% / 0.2%. Card rates 2.9%+$0.30 (Basic), 2.7%+$0.30 (Grow), 2.5%+$0.30 (Advanced). One source also lists a $5 Starter plan and a $1/month intro promo (sources disagree). — [Sellfy](https://sellfy.com/blog/shopify-pricing/), [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/small-business/shopify-pricing), [Shero Commerce](https://sherocommerce.com/shopify-pricing/)
- Etsy: no tiers for general sellers; optional Etsy Plus reported at $10/month (not confirmed on Etsy's page). — [Checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees)
- eBay Stores: store subscription lowers final value fee and adds free listings; sources conflict (Basic $21.95/mo annual with 1,000 free listings vs $4.95/mo with 250 listings). — [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/beginner-guides-how-tos/ebay-fees-for-stores-complete-2025-seller-guide), [EcomCalcTools](https://ecomcalctools.com/blog/ebay-store-guide/)
- Features gated by tier on Shopify (staff seats, reports, locations) were not extracted in this pass.

### Inferences
- Ladders in global ecommerce gate mainly by lower per-transaction fees and listing allowances, so the subscription is effectively a volume discount.

### Gaps
- Tier-by-tier feature gating (seats, analytics, ads credits, API, support, payout speed) not verified for any platform.
- Noon, Amazon, Jumia, Zalando, Veepee, Namshi, SHEIN, Temu: no subscription ladder found.

## 2. Commission ranges for fashion: Egypt/MENA and global

### Takeaway
Fashion in MENA commission-led marketplaces appears to sit roughly in the high teens to high 20s percent; global fashion typically 10-25%. Published Egypt-specific numbers are scarce and weakly sourced.

### Cited Findings
- Noon (UAE/KSA/Egypt combined): commission 4-27% by category; one report states clothes and footwear at 27%; weekly payouts; shipping/handling about 10% of sale price in one account. Not Egypt-specific. — [ChannelEngine](https://support.channelengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/16319074295197-noon-marketplace-guide), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/corporate-news/meydan-free-zone-dedicated-to-developing-e-commerce-entrepreneurs-with-onboarding-on-noon-1.1660291111034)
- Noon publishes an Egypt Fulfilled-by-Partner fee page (not retrieved, fetch timed out). — [Noon help centre](https://helpcenter.noon.partners/en/category/fulfilled-by-partner-fbp/fulfilled-by-partner-fbp-fees-in-egypt)
- Jumia: vendor page (April 2026 promo for international sellers) cites fashion/3C accessories commission cut from 20% to 18%; older sources 2.5-20% (2018); UNIDO study lists per-item processing fee EGP 5 (seller-held) or EGP 4 (Jumia warehouse), about 2021; a Kenya deck gives fashion 30% (not Egypt). — [Jumia vendor](https://vendor.jumia.com/?p=2180), [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/2018/12/marketplaces-2018-jumia/), [UNIDO](https://downloads.unido.org/ot/26/54/26543117/DigitalStudy_v2.pdf)
- Amazon Egypt: only a launch-era report of a minimum referral fee of 3%; no category rates found. US fashion for comparison (dated): clothing 17%, shoes 15-18% (2018 notice); EU promo 15% up to EUR 45 and 7% above. — [Scoop Empire](https://scoopempire.com/online-shopping-is-about-to-get-a-whole-lot-easier-with-amazon-eg-launching-later-this-year), [MyTotalRetail](https://www.mytotalretail.com/article/amazon-cuts-referral-fees-for-apparel-merchants)
- Zalando Partner Program: about 5-25% by category and price, charged only on completed non-returned orders, plus a small fixed monthly fee (third party). Shoes example 5% to EUR 20, 10% to 50, 20% to 100, 25% above (about 2021). — [Edock/Marketrock/Lengow via search](https://landing.marketrock.it/en/zalando-partner-program)
- SHEIN marketplace: about 10% (reports 8-12%; 12.24% UK), no entry fee, zero referral in first 30 days reported. Temu: 5-15% reported by Euromonitor; local-seller terms unverified. — [Shipsaving](https://www.shipsaving.com/blog/temu-shein-and-tiktok-shops-which-platform-is-best-for-us-ecommerce-sellers), [Euromonitor](https://www-euromonitor-com.lib.ezproxy.hkust.edu.hk/article/temu-shein-and-tiktok-shops-emerging-power-in-us-e-commerce)
- Etsy: $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction, about 3%+$0.25 payment processing, Offsite Ads 12-15% (capped $100). eBay clothing final value fee about 13.25% (12.35% with Store), alternative source 14.9%. — [Checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees), [Frooition](https://www.frooition.com/blog/ebay-fee-calculator-2026-the-professional-sellers-guide-to-profit-margins/)

### Inferences
- Noon's 27% fashion figure is likely the top of the MENA range; Jumia is mid-to-high teens; SHEIN/Etsy are low-end benchmarks.

### Gaps
- No primary Egypt rate cards for Noon, Jumia, Amazon.eg; Namshi marketplace terms not found.

## 3. Subscription+commission vs subscription-only vs commission-only: evidence

### Takeaway
No reliable churn or adoption evidence was found. Observed structure: commission-only dominates fashion marketplaces; hybrid exists at Etsy, eBay, Zalando (small fixed fee).

### Cited Findings
- Zalando: commission plus nominal monthly fee. — [Marketrock](https://landing.marketrock.it/en/zalando-partner-program)
- Veepee/Zalando Lounge: Veepee revenue described as primarily commission (job-listing source, weak) ; unsourced 20-40% consignment commission claim; Lounge reportedly works by consignment or firm purchase depending on campaign; Lounge requires separate contract. — [Vizologi](https://vizologi.com/business-strategy-canvas/veepee-business-model-canvas/), [Modaes](https://www.modaes.com/empresa/zalando-desafia-el-reino-de-privalia-lanza-su-outlet-online-en-espana), [Zalando partner](https://partner.zalando.com/university/article/introduction-to-lounge-by-zalando)
- Unverified claim of campaign minimums of 500-2,000 units and 28-56 day approval at Veepee (low-quality page; do not rely). — [Zunapro](https://www.zunapro.com/france/en/marketplace/veepee)

### Inferences
- Hybrid fits low-volume sellers who want cheaper marginal fees; commission-only suits cold-start platforms because no upfront cost.

### Gaps
- Churn, revenue-stability and small-vs-large adoption evidence: none found. Veepee/Namshi brand margins, margin guarantees: not found. Onboarding deposits, performance thresholds and penalties: not found (Zalando returns-netted commission only).

## 4. Pricing psychology for a cold-start marketplace

### Takeaway
Only competitor signals found: Jumia promo cut for international sellers, SHEIN 30-day zero-referral window, Temu invitation-only/no-fee local sellers, Amazon EU fee cuts in response to SHEIN/Temu.

### Cited Findings
- SHEIN reportedly waives referral fees for new sellers' first 30 days. — [Shipsaving](https://www.shipsaving.com/blog/temu-shein-and-tiktok-shops-which-platform-is-best-for-us-ecommerce-sellers)
- Amazon cut European seller fees amid SHEIN/Temu competition (Dec 2025). — [Modaes](https://www.modaes.com/global/companies/amazon-cuts-commissions-for-european-sellers-to-compete-with-shein-and-temu)
- Jumia temporary commission cut as seller-acquisition lever (April 2026). — [Jumia vendor](https://vendor.jumia.com/?p=2180)

### Inferences
- Observed options: fee holiday (30 days), time-boxed promotional rate, category-targeted cuts, success-only commission (Zalando charges on non-returned sales). Risks (margin erosion, adverse selection) are reasoning, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No empirical study on founding-partner pricing or free tiers found.
